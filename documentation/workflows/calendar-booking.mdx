---
title: "Calendar booking"
description: "Let an agent find free times and book appointments with Google Calendar, or send a Calendly link."
---

Agents can book appointments while they talk. Connect a calendar once for the workspace, then turn booking on for each agent that should use it.

## Connect a calendar

On **Connections**, use the **Google Calendar** card (**Connect Calendar**) or the **Calendly** card (**Connect Calendly**). Both sign in with your account. The card then shows when it was connected and when the sign-in expires.

| | Google Calendar | Calendly |
|---|---|---|
| What the agent does | Checks free times and books the slot itself | Sends the customer your scheduling link to pick a time |
| Calendar used | The primary calendar of the connected Google account | Your Calendly scheduling page |

## Turn booking on for an agent

On the agent's **Tools & Integrations** tab, turn on **Enable Booking**. In **Booking Instructions Prompt**, tell the agent how your bookings work. It has no other settings to go on:

```markdown
Appointments are 30 minutes. We take bookings Monday to Saturday,
10am to 7pm IST, up to two weeks ahead. Never book on Sundays.
Before booking, confirm the customer's name and the treatment they want.
Offer at most three times at once.
```

## How Google Calendar booking works

1. The agent checks free times between two dates it picks from the conversation. It finds every free 30-minute slot in that range.
2. It offers the customer a few of them.
3. Once the customer picks one, it creates the event in the primary calendar, with a title and an optional description.

Dates without a timezone are read in the agent's timezone, set on **Prompts & Identity**. Set it to your business's timezone.

There are no settings for working hours, buffers, or slot length. Free times come straight from the calendar. Spell out your hours in the booking instructions, or block unavailable times in Google Calendar itself.

<Warning>
The calendar isn't checked again at the moment of booking. If someone else takes the slot during the conversation, it can be double-booked. Keep conversations short between offering a time and booking it.
</Warning>

The customer doesn't get a calendar invite. If they need a confirmation, have the agent confirm the time in the conversation. On WhatsApp, a follow-up message can do the same after a call.

## Booking in workflows

In a workflow agent, call `booking` from a [Function step](/documentation/workflows/mid-call-rest-tools) when the details are collected, or make it available in a Conversation step.
