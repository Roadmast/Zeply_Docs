title: "Quickstart"
description: "Create a workspace, build your first agent, and talk to it."
---

This guide takes you from a new account to an agent you can talk to. Plan on about fifteen minutes, or longer if you also connect WhatsApp.

You need a browser with microphone access. To try the WhatsApp step, you also need a Meta business account and a phone number you can register with WhatsApp Business.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up with your email address, open the verification link we send you, then log in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your workspace">
    The first screen asks for a workspace name. A workspace holds your agents, contacts, inbox, and campaigns. You can create more workspaces later, for example one per brand or client.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create an agent">
    Open **Agents**, click **New Agent**, and choose **Prompt-based AI Agent**. Give it a welcome message, the first thing it says, and a system prompt that tells it who it is and what to do. For example:

    ```text
    You are the front desk for Green Leaf Dental. Answer questions about
    opening hours, prices, and treatments. If someone wants an appointment,
    collect their name and preferred day. Keep answers short. You are on a call.
    ```

    On **AI Model & Voice**, keep the Gemini stack, turn on **Enable voice**, and pick a voice. Click **Save Changes**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add knowledge (optional)">
    Upload a PDF, DOCX, or TXT file to the knowledge base, such as a price list or FAQ. Every agent in the workspace can search it when a question comes up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Talk to it">
    Click **Test Agent** and open the **Webcall** tab. Ask a question your prompt covers, then one only your uploaded file can answer. The **Chat** tab lets you test by typing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect WhatsApp">
    On **Connections**, connect your WhatsApp Business number and assign it to the agent. Send the number a message from your phone. The agent replies, the conversation shows up in **Inbox**, and the sender appears in **Contacts**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Browser calls are not saved to call logs. WhatsApp calls are, with their recording and transcript when recording is turned on for the agent.
</Note>
