## Send now or later

In the **Schedule** step, choose **Send Now (Immediate Launch)** to start as soon as you launch, or **Schedule for Later** and pick a time under **Select Launch Date & Time**.

You pick the time in your browser's local timezone, and Sayvy AI converts it for you. If the time you pick has already passed by the moment you launch, the campaign starts straight away.

A scheduled campaign can't be edited. To change its time, audience, or message, cancel it and create a new one.

## Pause, resume, and cancel

| Action | Works when the campaign is | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Pause | Scheduled, sending, or awaiting responses | Sending stops before the next contact. Messages already sent stay sent. No new calls are placed. |
| Resume | Paused | The campaign picks up where it stopped. Nobody who already got the message gets it again. If the scheduled time is still ahead, it goes back to waiting. |
| Cancel | Scheduled, sending, paused, or awaiting responses | Messages not yet sent are dropped. This can't be undone. |

Deleting a campaign removes it from your list.

Pausing is safe for urgent fixes, such as a typo found after launch. Pause, check how many messages went out, then cancel and send a corrected campaign to the rest.

Calls that are already in progress keep going when you pause or cancel a call campaign. Only new calls stop.

## Running a campaign again

Completed and cancelled campaigns can't be restarted or duplicated. To send the same message again, create a new campaign with the same template and audience. Contacts who got the earlier campaign will get the new one too.

## When campaigns pause on their own

A campaign can pause or move without you doing anything:

- A call campaign pauses when the workspace runs out of voice credits.
- If Meta reports a contact has reached their marketing message limit, the campaign is rescheduled for the next day.
- A scheduled template campaign is paused if the workspace moves to the Pay as you go plan.
