## In the campaign list

Each campaign on the **Campaigns** page shows four counts: sent, delivered, read, and failed. Next to them are the audience size, the scheduled time, and when it was created. Filter the list by status with the tabs above it.

## Campaign details

Open a campaign to see its status, total audience, launch time, and a delivery funnel:

| Stage | Counts messages that |
|---|---|
| Sent | Left Sayvy AI and were accepted by WhatsApp |
| Delivered | Reached the contact's phone |
| Read | Were opened. Contacts who turn off read receipts never show as read. |
| Failed | Couldn't be delivered |

A big gap between sent and delivered usually means many numbers aren't on WhatsApp, or are written wrongly. Few reads among delivered messages usually means the opening line isn't working.

## Call campaign numbers

The dashboard funnel covers messages only. For call campaigns, the [campaign API](/api-reference/campaigns/get-campaign) also returns how many contacts granted or rejected permission, and how many calls were started, answered, and completed. Each call's duration, recording, and transcript are in [call logs](/documentation/conversations/call-logs).

## PDF report

Download a PDF of a campaign to share with people who don't use Sayvy AI. It includes:

- the campaign name, status, audience, WhatsApp number, template, and dates (in UTC)
- the total audience and the sent, delivered, read, and failed counts
- delivery, read, and failure rates

The report covers messages only, not calls.
