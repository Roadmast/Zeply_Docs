---
title: "WhatsApp calls"
description: "Let your agent answer and place voice calls on your WhatsApp Business number."
---

Your agent can talk to customers over WhatsApp's own calling, on the same business number they already message. Customers call you from the WhatsApp app, and with their permission your agent can call them back.

## Set up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your number">
    On **Connections**, click **Connect WhatsApp** and finish signing in with your Meta business account. See [WhatsApp setup](/documentation/whatsapp/setup).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make sure calling is on for the number">
    WhatsApp Business calling has to be available and turned on for your number in Meta's settings. If it's off, calls never reach Sayvy AI.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign the number to a voice agent">
    On the agent's **Deploy** tab, turn on WhatsApp and choose the number. The agent needs **Enable voice** turned on and a voice picked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Call it">
    Call the number from WhatsApp on your phone. The agent should answer with its welcome message.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Calls from customers

Every call to the number goes to its agent, at any hour. See [Inbound calls](/documentation/deploy/inbound-calls) for how routing works and how to handle out-of-hours calls.

## Calls to customers

WhatsApp only lets a business call someone who has agreed to it. Outbound calls happen through [call campaigns](/documentation/campaigns/voice-campaigns), which you create through the API or MCP. They handle the permission step for you:

1. The campaign sends each contact a template message asking for permission to call.
2. When a contact accepts, the agent calls them.
3. Permission usually lasts seven days, unless the contact grants it permanently.

Meta limits how often you can ask. Each contact can get one permission request per 24 hours, and two per seven days.

## After the call

| What | When it's saved |
|---|---|
| Call log with status and duration | Always |
| Recording | When **Enable Voice / Call Recording** is on |
| Transcript | When **Enable Transcriptions** is on |
| Follow-up WhatsApp message | When **Enable Follow Ups** is on |
| Activity in the contact's history | Always |
| `call.started` and `call.completed` webhooks | Always, if you've set up a webhook |

Calls end after 15 minutes at most, whatever **Max Call Duration** is set to.
