---
title: "Voices"
description: "Choose the voice your agent speaks with on calls."
---

An agent's voice is set on the **AI Model & Voice** tab. Turn on **Enable voice**, then pick a voice from the list. The voices you see depend on the stack you chose above it.

| Stack | Voices | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Gemini | Google's prebuilt voices | English and other widely spoken languages |
| Sarvam | Sarvam's speakers, each with a style and gender | Indian languages and Indian English |

Voices belong to one stack. If you switch from Gemini to Sarvam, or back, the voice you picked is cleared and you choose again.

## Pick a voice

The picker shows six voices at a time. Search by name, description, or gender to narrow the list, and press play on any voice to hear a short sample before you choose. Try two or three with a line from your own script. A voice that sounds fine on a greeting can sound wrong reading out prices or addresses.

You can't save the agent with voice turned on and no voice selected.

## What you can't change yet

There are no controls for speed, pitch, or tone. To change how the agent sounds, pick a different voice, or adjust the wording in the prompt. Shorter sentences make any voice sound more relaxed. See [Writing for voice](/documentation/voice/persona-and-ssml).

## Cost

The **Voice Price** on the same tab shows what calls cost per minute, in credits, for the stack and category you chose. Minutes are taken from your workspace's prepaid credits.
