---
title: "Google Calendar"
description: "Connect Google Calendar so agents can find free times and book appointments."
---

Connecting Google Calendar lets your agents check when you're free and add appointments to your calendar during conversations.

## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Connections">
    Go to **Connections** and find the **Google Calendar** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with Google">
    Click **Connect Calendar** and sign in with the Google account that owns the calendar. Approve the access Google asks for, which covers viewing and creating calendar events.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn booking on for an agent">
    On the agent's **Tools & Integrations** tab, turn on **Enable Booking** and write its booking instructions. See [Calendar booking](/documentation/workflows/calendar-booking).
  </Step>
</Steps>

If connecting fails, you'll see "Failed to connect Google Calendar. Please try again." Try again with the right Google account.

## The calendar card

Once connected, the card shows when you connected, when the sign-in expires, and a **Real-time Booking** row.

<Note>
**Real-time Booking** always reads "Verified". It's a label, not a live test. Confirm booking works by asking a test agent to book a slot, then check your calendar.
</Note>

## Which calendar is used

Agents use the primary calendar of the connected Google account, both to look for free times and to add events. There's no setting to choose a different calendar, so connect the Google account whose primary calendar should hold your appointments.

## Disconnect

Click **Disconnect** on the card and confirm. Booking stops working for every agent until you reconnect. The warning reads "This will disable appointment booking workflows." Events already on your calendar stay.

## If bookings stop working

Agents that can't reach the calendar fail with "Failed to retrieve calendar availability. Please try again." Reconnect the calendar. The sign-in may have expired. See [Troubleshooting](/documentation/go-live/troubleshooting).
