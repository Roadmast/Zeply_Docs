Most integrations are connected on one page, **Connections**, in the dashboard. Each has a card that shows its connection details and the buttons to update or disconnect it.

## Channels

| Integration | What it does | Details |
|---|---|---|
| WhatsApp Business | Messages and voice calls on your business number | [WhatsApp setup](/documentation/whatsapp/setup) |
| Website Chatbot | Chat and voice on your own site | [Web calls and widget](/documentation/voice/web-audio-webrtc) |
| Instagram Direct | Receive and answer Instagram messages in the inbox | [Channels](/documentation/inbox/channels) |
| Email Channel | Bring a mailbox into the inbox through forwarding | [Channels](/documentation/inbox/channels) |

## Payments

Razorpay processes subscription and credit payments. It isn't something you connect. It appears at checkout. See [Billing](/documentation/platform/billing).

## What isn't available yet

- Bringing your own AI model, speech, or telephony accounts. Sayvy AI supplies the models, so voice and chat run on the Gemini and Sarvam presets you choose per agent.
- Regular phone numbers and SIP trunks. Voice runs in the browser and over WhatsApp.
- A direct connection to external CRMs. Use the REST API or webhooks to keep one in sync.
- Ready-made connectors for tools such as Zapier or Slack. Webhooks and the API work with them, but you set the connection up yourself.
