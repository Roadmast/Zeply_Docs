---
title: "Calendly"
description: "Connect Calendly so agents can send customers your scheduling link."
---

Calendly works differently from Google Calendar. An agent doesn't book the slot itself. It gives the customer your Calendly scheduling link, and the customer picks a time on Calendly's page.

Choose Calendly when you already run your schedule there, or when appointments need Calendly's own rules, such as buffers, round-robin hosts, or intake questions.

## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Connections">
    Go to **Connections** and find the **Calendly** card. It reads "Connect Calendly to send appointment booking links via WhatsApp."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with Calendly">
    Click **Connect Calendly** and sign in with your Calendly account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn booking on for an agent">
    On the agent's **Tools & Integrations** tab, turn on **Enable Booking**, and tell the agent in its booking instructions to send the link. See [Calendar booking](/documentation/workflows/calendar-booking).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The Calendly card

The card shows when you connected, when the sign-in expires, and your Calendly slug if there is one.

## What the agent does

When a customer wants to book, the agent sends your scheduling link in the conversation. The agent doesn't see which time the customer chooses, because that happens on Calendly's page.

If you need the agent to confirm the booking, ask the customer to tell it when they've booked.

## Disconnect

Click **Disconnect** and confirm. The warning reads that it will disable appointment booking workflows. Agents stop being able to send your link until you reconnect.

## Google Calendar or Calendly

| | Google Calendar | Calendly |
|---|---|---|
| Who picks the time | The agent and the customer, in conversation | The customer, on Calendly |
| Who creates the event | The agent | Calendly |
| Suits | Simple appointments you want handled in chat | Schedules with rules or several hosts |
