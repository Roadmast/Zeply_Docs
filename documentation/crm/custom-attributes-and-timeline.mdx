---
title: "Custom fields"
description: "Store your own details on a contact as key and value pairs."
---

Custom fields hold details about a contact that the standard fields don't cover: a policy number, a preferred branch, a birthday, an email address. Each one is a key, such as `branch`, with a value, such as `Baner`.

There's no list of fields to set up first. Add whatever keys you need on each contact. Different contacts can have different fields.

## Add or edit custom fields

Custom fields are edited from the inbox. Open a conversation with the contact, open the customer profile, and click **Edit Contact Details**. Under **Custom Fields**, click **Add Field**, enter a key and value, then **Save Details**.

The **Email** field in that form is stored as a custom field called `email`.

## Keep keys consistent

Because fields aren't defined in advance, nothing stops one teammate typing `Branch` and another `branch_name`. That matters more than it seems: campaign variables and API integrations look fields up by their exact key, so a contact with `Branch` won't get the value meant for `branch`. Write down the keys your team uses, in lowercase with underscores.

## Fields Sayvy AI adds

Contacts from Instagram get a few fields filled in automatically, such as their Instagram username and display name.

## Where custom fields are used

| Place | How |
|---|---|
| WhatsApp campaigns | A template variable can take its value from a custom field |
| Inbox profile | Shown and edited under **Custom Fields** |
| API | Read and written on the contact object |

Agents can't read or change custom fields during a conversation yet.

## Things to know

- Custom fields can't be imported from a spreadsheet. The import only reads phone number, name, and tags.
- When you update custom fields through the API, send the full set. The fields you send replace the old ones rather than being merged in.
