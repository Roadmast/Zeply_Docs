A contact is one person your business talks to. Most contacts are created automatically when someone first reaches you. This page covers working with them by hand in **CRM Hub**.

## Add a contact

Click **New Contact** and enter a **Full Name**, a **WhatsApp Phone Number**, and any **Tags**. The phone number must be unique in the workspace. If another contact already has it, the new one isn't created.

Add the rest of the details, such as notes, stage, and custom fields, after the contact is saved. To add many contacts at once, see [Import and export](/documentation/crm/import-export).

## Find contacts

The table lists each contact's name, phone, stage, lead score, handling, and tags. The most recently active contacts come first.

- Search matches on name and phone number.
- **Handling** filters to contacts handled by AI or by a human.
- **Score** filters to high (70 to 100), medium (40 to 69), or low (0 to 39) lead scores.
- The stage cards at the top of the page filter by lifecycle stage.

The **Kanban** view lays contacts out in columns by stage, which helps when you want to see how many sit at each step.

## Open a contact

Click **View Profile** on a row to open the contact. From there you can:

- change the **Lifecycle Stage** and **Lead Score**
- switch **Conversation Handling** between AI and a human
- edit tags
- read the **Activity Timeline** and add notes
- add and complete **Tasks & Reminders**

**Chat in Inbox** jumps to the contact's conversation, and **Edit Contact** changes their name, phone, and tags.

## Contact details

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Name | Required |
| Phone number | Required and unique in the workspace. For Instagram, email, and web widget contacts this holds a channel ID instead. See [Identity resolution](/documentation/memory/identity-resolution). |
| Tags | Any labels you like. Campaigns can target contacts by tag. |
| Notes | One running notes field. Each note added by a teammate or the AI is appended on a new line. |
| Custom fields | Your own key and value pairs. See [Custom fields](/documentation/crm/custom-attributes-and-timeline). |
| Lifecycle stage | New, engaged, qualified, customer, or lost. New contacts start as new. |
| Lead score | 0 to 100. New contacts start at 0. |
| Owner | The teammate responsible for the contact |
| Source | The channel the contact first came from |

## Delete a contact

**Delete Contact** removes the contact for good, together with their activity history, tasks, and inbox conversations. It can't be undone. If you only want to stop messaging someone, tag them instead.
