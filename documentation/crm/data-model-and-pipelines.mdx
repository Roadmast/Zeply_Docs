---
title: "CRM data model"
description: "How contacts, conversations, activities, and tasks relate to each other."
---

The CRM is built around the contact. Everything else, from conversations to tasks, hangs off a contact and belongs to one workspace. This page is useful if you work with the CRM through the API or webhooks.

## Objects

| Object | What it holds | Belongs to |
|---|---|---|
| Contact | Name, phone number or channel ID, tags, notes, custom fields, stage, score, owner, handling, source | The workspace |
| Inbox ticket | One conversation between the contact and one of your channels, open or closed | A contact |
| Message | A single message in a ticket, in either direction | A ticket |
| Activity | One event in the contact's history, made by a user, the AI, or the system | A contact |
| Task | A follow-up with a title, description, due date, assignee, and status | A contact |

A contact can have many tickets, one per channel conversation. When a ticket is closed and the person writes again, a new ticket opens.

## Lifecycle stages

There's one fixed set of stages. You can't add, rename, or remove stages.

| Stage | Typical meaning |
|---|---|
| `new` | Just arrived. Every contact starts here. |
| `engaged` | In conversation with you |
| `qualified` | A real prospect with a need you can meet |
| `customer` | Has bought from you |
| `lost` | Not going ahead |

The meanings are yours to decide. Write them into your agent's prompt so the agent moves contacts the same way your team would.

## Lead score

The lead score is a whole number from 0 to 100, starting at 0. It isn't calculated for you. It changes only when a teammate sets it, or when an agent with CRM tools updates it. Score bands in CRM Hub treat 70 and above as high and below 40 as low.

## Deals

The CRM doesn't have deals, amounts, or multiple pipelines. To track value or product interest, use tags or custom fields on the contact.

## Deleting

Deleting a contact also deletes its tickets, messages, activities, and tasks. See [Contacts](/documentation/crm/contacts).
