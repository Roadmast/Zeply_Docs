Sayvy AI has a CRM built in, so every person who talks to your agents ends up as a contact you can follow up with. Open it from **CRM Hub** in the dashboard.

Contacts are created for you. The first time someone messages your WhatsApp number, writes on Instagram, emails your connected address, or chats in your web widget, a contact appears. You can also add contacts by hand or upload a spreadsheet.

## What the CRM tracks

| | What it is |
|---|---|
| Contact | One person, identified by their phone number or channel ID, with tags, notes, and custom fields |
| Lifecycle stage | Where they stand: new, engaged, qualified, customer, or lost |
| Lead score | A number from 0 to 100 for how promising they are |
| Handling | Whether AI replies to them, or a person on your team does |
| Activity | An entry in their history, such as a message, a stage change, or a note |
| Task | A follow-up for your team, with a due date |

## How agents keep it current

With CRM tools turned on in the agent's **Tools & Integrations** tab, the agent updates the CRM while it talks. It can move a contact to a new stage, change their lead score, add a note, create a follow-up task, or hand the contact to your team. Each change is logged in the contact's activity history, marked as made by the AI.

Nothing changes stage or score on its own. Without an agent or a teammate making the change, a contact stays where it is. Tell your agent in its prompt when to update them:

```markdown
When the caller confirms a budget and a timeline, set their stage to
qualified and their lead score to at least 70. Add a note with both.
```

## The CRM Hub page

Cards across the top count all contacts and those in each stage. Click a card to filter the list by that stage. Below them, switch between three views: **Table**, **Kanban** (columns by stage), and **Tasks**.
