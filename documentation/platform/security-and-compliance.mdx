## Signing in

You can sign in with an email and password, or with Google. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider, not stored by Sayvy AI itself.

### Two-factor sign-in

Add a second step with an authenticator app, such as Google Authenticator. Go to **Settings**, then **Security**, click **Setup Google Authenticator**, scan the code, and enter the six-digit code under **Verify & Enable**. Ask every admin to turn it on.

## Access control

Everyone on your team has a role, and each workspace is separate. Work in one workspace never appears in another. Only admins can invite people, change roles, and remove members. See [Team members](/documentation/workspace/organizations-and-team-members).

Invitations expire after 7 days, and the last admin of an organization can't be removed.

## How your data is protected

- **Connection secrets** are encrypted before they're stored. This covers the tokens for WhatsApp, Instagram, Google Calendar, and Calendly, and the secrets used to sign your webhooks.
- **API keys** are stored only as a hash, so a database leak wouldn't expose them. See [API keys](/documentation/platform/api-keys-and-security).
- **Webhook requests** are signed, so your server can check where they came from. See [Webhook delivery and security](/documentation/workflows/outbound-webhooks-dlq).

## What to keep in mind

Agents see whatever you give them, and callers can ask them anything. A few habits keep that safe:

- Don't put passwords, card numbers, or other secrets in an agent prompt or knowledge base document.
- Tell agents in the prompt what they must never disclose, such as other customers' details.
- Recordings and transcripts contain what callers said. Turn them off for agents where you don't need them, and tell callers they're being recorded where the law requires it.
- Remove ex-teammates from **Team Management** straight away.

## What isn't available yet

| | Status |
|---|---|
| Single sign-on (SAML) | Not available |
| Audit log of who changed what | Not available |
| Deleting your account or a workspace from the dashboard | Not available. Contact support. |
| Choosing where your data is hosted | Not available |

If your organization needs formal certifications, a data processing agreement, or a security questionnaire answered, contact support. Don't rely on this page for compliance claims.
