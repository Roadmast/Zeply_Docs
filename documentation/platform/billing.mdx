Sayvy AI bills in two parts. A monthly or yearly **subscription** unlocks features and sets your limits. **Credits** pay for what you use: voice minutes and AI replies. Billing belongs to your organization, so it covers every workspace in it.

Everything is charged in Indian rupees through Razorpay. Prices on the page are shown plus 18% GST.

## Subscribe to a plan

Open **Billing** from the sidebar and go to **Subscription Plans**. Switch between **Monthly** and **Yearly**, where yearly billing saves 20%, then click **Subscribe** on the plan you want. Razorpay's checkout takes the payment.

New organizations get a 7-day free trial. When it ends, feature pages are paused until you subscribe or move to Pay as you go. Toward the end of the trial, and after it, monthly plans get a 30% discount on the first month.

Buying a new plan ends the old subscription straight away. There's no proration, so change plans at the start of a billing cycle if you can. The dashboard has no cancel button yet. When a subscription is cancelled, the organization drops to Pay as you go, and its credits stay.

## Credits

Credits are the balance shown at the top of the billing page and in the sidebar.

To top up, go to **Top-Up Credits**, choose an amount with the slider or a quick pick, and click **Buy Credits**. The order summary shows the credit value, any discount, GST, and the total.

| | |
|---|---|
| Price | ₹1 per credit, plus 18% GST |
| Minimum | 500 credits |
| Maximum | 25,000 credits per top-up |
| Volume discount | 5% off from 10,000 credits, and 10% off at 25,000 |
| Expiry | Credits never expire |

### Voice rates

Voice is charged by the minute, in 30-second blocks, at the same rate on every plan.

| Gemini preset | Credits per minute | Sarvam preset | Credits per minute |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lite | 4.50 | Lite | 4.00 |
| Flash | 5.99 | GLM | 4.75 |
| Live | 6.99 | Flash | 4.99 |
| Pro | 8.99 | Pro | 6.49 |
| Live Reasoning | 9.99 | Max | 7.99 |

### AI reply rates

Each reply the AI writes in chat costs a small number of credits, depending on the preset.

| Gemini preset | Credits per reply | Sarvam preset | Credits per reply |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lite | 0.15 | Lite | 0.03 |
| Flash | 0.27 | GLM | 0.05 |
| Live | 0.27 | Flash | 0.05 |
| Live Reasoning | 0.27 | Pro | 0.39 |
| Pro | 0.76 | Max | 0.79 |

### When credits run out

AI replies stop working, new calls are rejected, and a call in progress ends when it can't renew. Call campaigns pause. The dashboard doesn't warn you first, so top up before a big campaign, and check the balance in the sidebar.

## Receipts

**Invoices** lists your payments under three tabs: **Plans**, **Add-ons**, and **AI Credits**. Download any of them as a PDF.

These are payment receipts. They aren't GST tax invoices, and they don't include a GSTIN.
