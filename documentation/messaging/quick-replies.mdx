---
title: "Quick replies"
description: "Save messages your team sends often and insert them with a slash shortcut."
---

Quick replies are saved messages your team can drop into any conversation. They're useful for answers you type again and again: opening hours, payment details, directions, a polite sign-off.

Quick replies are shared across the workspace, so everyone on the team sees the same list.

## Create one

Go to **Message Library** then **Quick Replies**, and click **New Quick Reply**. Fill in two fields:

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| Shortcut | A short trigger word, such as `/hours` or `/upi`. Up to 100 characters. |
| Message Content | The full message to insert |

```text
Shortcut:  /hours
Message:   We're open 9am to 7pm, Monday to Saturday. On Sundays
           we open from 10am to 2pm.
```

Quick replies are text only. To send a file, attach it from the composer.

## Use one in a conversation

In the inbox composer, type `/`. A list opens. Keep typing and it narrows, matching both shortcuts and the text of each message, so `/pay` would also find a reply that mentions payment. Pick one with the arrow keys, then press Enter or Tab. Esc closes it.

The message replaces what you typed and stays in the composer, so you can change it before sending. You can also open the list with the **Insert Quick Reply** button.

## Change or remove one

Quick replies can't be edited. To change one, delete it and create it again with the same shortcut.
