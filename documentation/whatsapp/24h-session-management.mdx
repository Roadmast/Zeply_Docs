---
title: "The 24-hour window"
description: "When you can send free-form WhatsApp messages, and what to do when the window closes."
---

WhatsApp only lets a business send free-form messages for 24 hours after the customer's last message. This is called the customer service window. Inside it, you and your agent can reply however you like. Outside it, you can only send a template that Meta has approved.

## How it works

Every message from a customer opens or restarts a 24-hour window. Replies inside the window are free-form: text, images, documents, and so on. When 24 hours pass with no message from the customer, the window closes.

Starting a conversation yourself, with a customer who hasn't written recently, always needs a template. Once the customer replies, the window opens and you can write freely again.

## What Sayvy AI does

Sayvy AI doesn't check the window for you. Neither the inbox composer nor the AI agent stops a message from being sent after 24 hours. WhatsApp rejects it, and you see an error instead of a delivered message.

| Where | What happens after 24 hours |
|---|---|
| Inbox composer | The send fails and the composer shows an error |
| AI agent | Replies come only after a customer message, so they're always in the window |
| Campaigns | Always use templates, so the window doesn't apply |

## What to do when the window has closed

Send an approved template. You can send one from the inbox with the template button beside the search box, or in bulk with a [WhatsApp campaign](/documentation/campaigns/whatsapp-campaigns). When the customer replies, you're back inside the window.

Keep a few templates ready for common cases: following up on a quote, reminding someone of an appointment, or asking if they still need help. See [Message templates](/documentation/messaging/templates).

## Tips

- Reply to customers within the day when you can. It keeps the conversation free-form.
- If your team often takes over from the AI, check older tickets each morning for ones nearing 24 hours.
- Calls follow separate rules. See [WhatsApp calls](/documentation/voice/whatsapp-calls).
