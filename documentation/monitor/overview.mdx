Once agents are live, check on them regularly. Listen to calls, read conversations, and watch the numbers that matter for your business. Sayvy AI gives you four places to look.

| Where | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| [Call logs](/documentation/conversations/call-logs) | Every WhatsApp call, with its recording and transcript |
| [Analytics](/documentation/platform/analytics-and-usage) | Call, message, and campaign trends, per agent and over time |
| [Workflow executions](/documentation/workflows/overview) | Where callers drop off in a workflow agent |
| [Webhooks](/documentation/monitor/webhooks-and-events) | Live events sent to your own systems for alerts and reports |

The [inbox](/documentation/conversations/inbox) and the contacts [activity timeline](/documentation/crm/timeline) show conversations one at a time.

## A weekly review

A short routine catches most problems before customers complain:

1. Open **Analytics** and compare this week's call success rate and message delivery rate with last week's.
2. In **Call Logs**, filter by failed and missed calls, and look for a pattern in time of day or number.
3. Listen to three or four completed calls from start to finish. Note where the agent was slow, repeated itself, or misunderstood.
4. For workflow agents, open **Executions** and find the step with the highest drop-off.
5. Change one thing in the prompt or workflow, then check the same numbers next week.

## Turn on recording first

Call logs only have recordings and transcripts for agents that have **Enable Voice / Call Recording** and **Enable Transcriptions** turned on in Advanced Settings. Turn them on before going live, because calls can't be recorded after they happen.
