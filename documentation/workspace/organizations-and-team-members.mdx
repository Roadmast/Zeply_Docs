## Organizations and workspaces

Your organization is your business account. It owns the plan, the credit balance, and the seats. Inside it sit workspaces, which hold agents, contacts, the inbox, and campaigns. Nothing in one workspace shows up in another, so they suit separate brands or client accounts.

A person can belong to only one organization. When you sign up, onboarding creates yours along with a first workspace named after it, and makes you the admin.

To add a workspace, open the workspace menu in the sidebar and choose **Create Workspace**. Use the same menu to switch between them. Workspaces can't be renamed or deleted from the dashboard yet.

## Invite a teammate

Go to **Settings**, then **Team Management**, and click **Invite Member**. Enter their **Full Name** and **Business Email**, choose a role, and send the invite.

They get an email with a link that works for 7 days. Opening it lets them set a password and join straight away. Link expired before they got to it? Use **Reinvite** on their row. **Cancel** withdraws an invite nobody has accepted.

The **Workspace Roster** lists everyone with their role, status, and when they joined or were invited.

## Roles

| Role | What it's for |
|---|---|
| Admin | Full control of team members, workspace settings, and billing |
| Member | Day-to-day work on campaigns, inbox tickets, contacts, and agents |
| Viewer | Read-only access to call logs, analytics, and reports |

Only admins can invite people, change roles, or remove members. An organization always needs one admin, so the last admin can't be removed or demoted.

To change someone's role or remove them, use the **Role** and **Remove** actions on their row.

## Seat limits

Your plan sets how many team members you can have, counted across the whole organization.

| Plan | Seats |
|---|---|
| Pay as you go | 3 |
| Basic | 3 |
| Advanced | 6 |
| Premium | 10 |
| Enterprise | Set by your agreement |

When you reach the limit, the invite form says "Seat limit reached" with an **Upgrade plan** link. Extra seats cost ₹299 a month each. See [Plans and limits](/documentation/platform/plans-and-limits).

## Your own profile

Your name, email, and active workspace are shown under **Settings** then **User Profile**. Change your password from the avatar menu. Turn on two-factor sign-in under **Settings** then **Security**. See [Security and compliance](/documentation/platform/security-and-compliance).
