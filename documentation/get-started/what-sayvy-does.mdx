Sayvy AI gives your business AI agents that hold real conversations with customers, by voice or by text, and record every one of them in a built-in CRM. You write the instructions, pick a model and a voice, and connect the agent to the channels your customers already use.

## What an agent can do

An agent can answer a WhatsApp call, talk to a visitor on your website, or reply to a message in your inbox. While it talks, it can search your knowledge base, check your Google Calendar or Calendly for a free slot and book it, add a note to the contact, or create a follow-up task. When a question needs a person, it hands the conversation to your team.

## Where conversations happen

| Channel | Voice | Messages |
|---|---|---|
| Website widget | Browser calls | Chat |
| WhatsApp | WhatsApp Business calling | Chat and templates |
| Instagram | No | Direct messages |
| Email | No | Email threads |

<Note>
Calls over regular phone numbers are not available yet. Voice works in the browser and over WhatsApp. Agents reply to messages on WhatsApp and the website widget. Instagram and email conversations go to your team in the inbox.
</Note>

## Everything around the agent

Agents are one part of the platform. The others work with them:

- The inbox lists every conversation, so a teammate can read along, take over, or reply.
- Contacts keep each customer's details, tags, lifecycle stage, lead score, notes, and history. Agents update them during conversations.
- Campaigns send a WhatsApp template to many contacts at once, or have an agent call each of them.
- The knowledge base stores the PDF, DOCX, and TXT files agents search for answers.
- Webhooks tell your own systems when a call starts or ends, when a message arrives, and when an agent uses a tool.
