---
title: "AI to human handoff"
description: "How conversations pass from your agent to your team, and back again."
---

Your agent can handle a conversation from start to finish, but some customers need a person. A handoff stops the AI replying to a contact so your team can take over, and a hand-back lets the AI pick up again.

## When the agent hands off

An agent with CRM tools turned on can hand a conversation to a human whenever it decides it should. Tell it when, in its prompt:

```markdown
Hand the conversation to a human if the customer asks for a person,
is upset, wants a refund, or asks something you can't answer from the
knowledge base. Tell them a team member will reply soon.
```

Workflow agents hand off with a transfer step.

## What a handoff does

- AI stops replying to that contact.
- The contact can be assigned to a teammate as its owner.
- A handoff entry, with the agent's reason, is added to the contact's history.
- When a workflow agent transfers, an `agent.handoff` webhook is sent, if you've subscribed to it.

<Warning>
Nobody on your team is notified inside Sayvy AI when a handoff happens. Watch the inbox, or connect the `agent.handoff` webhook to Slack, email, or another tool your team checks.
</Warning>

## Taking over by hand

You don't need to wait for the agent. In any conversation, switch **AI On** to **AI Off** in the header. The AI won't reply on that ticket until you switch it back, even if it was halfway through writing a reply.

Replying yourself doesn't turn the AI off. If AI is still on, the agent may answer the customer's next message as well. Switch it off before you join in.

## Handing back to AI

When your part is done, switch the ticket back to **AI On**. If the contact had been handed off, this turns AI handling back on for them and records the hand-back in their history.

You can also switch a contact between **AI Handled** and **Human Agent** from their row in the contacts list.

## How AI on and off fit together

AI replies to a message only when both are true:

1. AI is on for the ticket.
2. The contact is set to AI handling, meaning they haven't been handed off.

A handoff turns off the second one for all of the contact's tickets. The header toggle controls the first one for a single ticket.
