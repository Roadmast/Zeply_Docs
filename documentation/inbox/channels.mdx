Channels are connected on the **Connections** page. Each one has its own card, and once connected the card shows the account details along with buttons to update or disconnect.

| Channel | AI replies | Voice | What you need |
|---|---|---|---|
| WhatsApp Business | Yes | Yes | A Meta business account and a number registered for the WhatsApp Business Platform |
| Website Chatbot | Yes | Yes | Access to your website's HTML |
| Instagram Direct | No | No | An Instagram professional account |
| Email Channel | No | No | An email inbox that can auto-forward, such as Gmail or Outlook |

## WhatsApp Business

Click **Connect WhatsApp** and sign in with Meta. Choose your business and the number to connect, and you're sent back to Sayvy AI. The card then shows the phone number and connection date. Use **Update Token** if Meta asks you to sign in again.

Disconnecting pauses campaigns and AI replies that use the number. See [WhatsApp setup](/documentation/whatsapp/setup) for details.

## Website Chatbot

Click **Configure Chatbot** to set the widget's title, greeting, suggested questions, and colours, then copy the embed script onto your site. See [Web calls and widget](/documentation/voice/web-audio-webrtc).

## Instagram Direct

Click **Connect Instagram** and sign in with your Instagram professional account. You don't need to link a Facebook page. Direct messages then arrive in the inbox, and your team can reply with text, images, video, or audio. A workspace can connect one Instagram account.

## Email Channel

Email works by forwarding. Sayvy AI gives you an address, and your mailbox forwards incoming mail to it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your address">
    Click **Connect Email**, enter the address customers write to, and continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up forwarding">
    In Gmail or Outlook, add an auto-forwarding rule to the forwarding address Sayvy AI shows you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm it">
    Your mail provider sends a confirmation email to that address. Sayvy AI catches it and shows you the confirmation link or code, so you can approve forwarding without leaving the page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Replies you send from the inbox keep the subject line, so they stay in the customer's existing email thread. You can attach one file per reply.
