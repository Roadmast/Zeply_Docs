title: "Inbox"
description: "Read and answer every customer conversation in one place, alongside your AI agent."
---

The inbox is where your team sees every conversation from WhatsApp, Instagram, email, and the web widget. Each conversation is a ticket: one contact on one of your channels. New messages appear on their own, without a page refresh.

## The conversation list

Tabs at the top switch between **All**, **Open**, and **Closed** tickets. The search box filters by contact name or phone number. Each row shows who the contact is, which channel they're on, the time of the last message, and whether the ticket is open.

To message someone who hasn't written to you, use the template button beside the search box. It opens **Start New Conversation**, where you pick a contact and an approved WhatsApp template.

## Inside a conversation

The header holds the controls for the ticket:

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **AI On** / **AI Off** | Turns automatic AI replies on or off for this ticket. See [AI to human handoff](/documentation/inbox/ai-human-handoff). |
| Close / reopen | Closes the ticket when the conversation is done, or reopens it. |
| Contact details | Opens the customer profile beside the conversation. |

Hover over a message to reply to it with a quote, or to react with one of eight emoji. Reactions work on WhatsApp only.

## Replying

Type in the composer and press Enter to send. Shift+Enter starts a new line. Type `/` to search your [quick replies](/documentation/messaging/quick-replies), and use the paperclip to attach one file. Images, video, and audio are sent as media. Anything else is sent as a document.

<Warning>
WhatsApp only accepts free-form replies within 24 hours of the customer's last message. The composer doesn't stop you from replying later. If WhatsApp rejects the message, you'll see an error. To restart an old conversation, send a template instead.
</Warning>

Your own messages show a clock while sending, two ticks once read, and one tick otherwise.

## The customer profile

The profile panel shows the contact's name, phone or email, tags, notes, and custom fields. Click **Edit Contact Details** to change them without leaving the conversation. Under **Actions & User History** you can see every earlier ticket with this contact, or close and reopen the current one.

## What the AI answers

When AI is on, the agent answers text messages on WhatsApp and the web widget. It doesn't answer voice notes, images, or other media. It doesn't reply on Instagram or email either, so your team handles those.
