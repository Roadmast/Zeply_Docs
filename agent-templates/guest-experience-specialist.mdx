# Guest Experience Specialist

> Delivers 24/7 guest service for hotels and resorts — handles room service requests, answers amenity questions, and gathers post-stay feedback.

The **Guest Experience Specialist** elevates the guest journey from arrival to checkout. Placed on in-room phones or web concierge portals, it handles service requests (extra towels, late checkout, luggage storage), provides local dining tips, and conducts post-stay satisfaction follow-ups.

## Key capabilities

* **In-Stay Request Triage**: Dispatches housekeeping, maintenance, extra key, and room amenity requests.
* **Resort Dining & Activity Coordination**: Recommends on-site restaurants, spa treatments, pool hours, and gym facilities.
* **Checkout & Transportation Assistance**: Handles luggage assistance requests, taxi or airport shuttle bookings, and late checkout inquiries.
* **Post-Stay NPS & Feedback Collection**: Conducts friendly post-stay satisfaction calls and logs guest feedback.

## Recommended tools & integrations

* **Property Management Systems (PMS)**: Sync room numbers, guest names, and service requests directly to Opera or Cloudbeds.
* **Knowledge Base**: Store room dining menus, resort maps, concierge recommendations, and check-in/out policies.
* **Service Ticketing**: Send instant alerts to housekeeping and maintenance teams.
