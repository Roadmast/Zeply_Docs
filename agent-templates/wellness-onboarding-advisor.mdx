## Key capabilities

* **Goal Discovery**: Uncovers health targets such as weight management, muscle building, stress reduction, or rehabilitation.
* **Assessment & Class Scheduling**: Books baseline fitness evaluations, body composition scans, or group class trials.
* **Club Guidelines & Amenity Overviews**: Informs members about operating hours, guest passes, locker policies, and mobile app perks.
* **Early Habit Reinforcement**: Checks in on initial workout frequency and removes friction for newcomers.

## Recommended tools & integrations

* **Fitness Management Software**: Integrate with Mindbody, ABC Fitness, or Glofox.
* **Calendar Booking**: Direct trainer schedule synchronization.
* **Knowledge Base**: Store class schedules, personal trainer profiles, and gym rules.
