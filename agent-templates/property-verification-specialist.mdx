## Key capabilities

* **Buyer Intent & Requirement Mapping**: Discovers desired bedrooms (BHK), square footage, amenities, and move-in timeline.
* **Budget & Pre-Approval Checks**: Determines price range and whether home loan financing is already pre-approved.
* **Site Visit Booking**: Schedules in-person property walkthroughs with assigned listing agents.
* **Property Highlights & Amenities**: Shares details about schools, metro access, clubhouse amenities, and possession dates.

## Recommended tools & integrations

* **Real Estate CRM**: Sync qualified lead records and notes directly to Salesforce, HubSpot, or LeadSquared.
* **Calendar Scheduler**: Synchronize showing times with property sales associates.
* **Knowledge Base**: Ingest property brochures, floor plans, pricing sheets, and master development layouts.
