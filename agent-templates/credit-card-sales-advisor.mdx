## Key capabilities

* **Lifestyle Spending Profiling**: Identifies whether the customer spends most on travel, dining, fuel, or online shopping.
* **Reward & Benefit Breakdown**: Articulates cashback multipliers, airport lounge access, reward point values, and welcome gifts.
* **Fee Transparency & Waiver Criteria**: Explains annual maintenance fees and annual spend milestones for 100% fee waivers.
* **Fast Pre-Approval Capture**: Gathers contact details, employment status, and consent for credit bureau checks.

## Recommended tools & integrations

* **Card Comparison API**: Dynamically fetch current promotions, joining perks, and card tier requirements.
* **Knowledge Base**: Add card benefit brochures, reward rate sheets, and insurance terms.
* **CRM Sync**: Sync hot leads directly to your sales pipeline for document pickup or instant e-KYC.
