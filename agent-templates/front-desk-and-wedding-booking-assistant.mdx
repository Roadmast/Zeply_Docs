## Key capabilities

* **Venue & Package Consultation**: Delivers transparent details on guest capacities, decor options, catering menus, and pricing tiers.
* **Tour Scheduling**: Books venue walkthroughs with prospective couples and integrates with Google Calendar or Calendly.
* **General Front Desk Q&A**: Answers common questions on check-in policies, parking, accommodation discounts, and amenities.
* **Warm Human Escalation**: Seamlessly routes complex custom inquiries or high-priority planners to on-duty sales directors.

## Recommended tools & integrations

* **Google Calendar / Calendly**: Automatically reserve tour slots and consultation calls.
* **Knowledge Base**: Upload venue brochures, catering menus, floor plans, and seasonal pricing sheets.
* **CRM / Webhooks**: Stream collected contact details, guest counts, and target wedding dates to your event CRM.
