# Salon Receptionist

> Manages salon bookings around the clock — schedules haircuts, coloring, spa services, checks stylist availability, and handles rescheduling.

The **Salon Receptionist** ensures your salon or spa never misses a booking call, even during busy peak hours or after closing time. It effortlessly details service menus, pricing, duration, stylist specialties, and locks in appointment reservations into your salon calendar.

## Key capabilities

* **Service Menu & Price Consultation**: Explains packages, add-ons (keratin, deep conditioning), durations, and costs.
* **Stylist Matching & Calendar Booking**: Checks availability for preferred stylists or recommends open openings.
* **Rescheduling & Cancellation Management**: Accommodates date/time changes and enforces 24-hour cancellation policies politely.
* **Appointment Reminders**: Sends confirmation texts and preparation guidelines (e.g. hair wash instructions).

## Recommended tools & integrations

* **Salon Booking Software**: Connect Boulevard, Vagaro, Fresha, Mindbody, or Google Calendar.
* **Knowledge Base**: Upload service menus, stylist biographies, address details, and parking notes.
* **SMS Confirmation**: Send booking confirmation texts automatically upon reservation.
