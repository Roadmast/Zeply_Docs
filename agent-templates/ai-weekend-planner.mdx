## Key capabilities

* **Preference Discovery**: Understands dietary preferences, relaxation vs. adventure style, family-friendly needs, and budgets.
* **Curated Itinerary Generation**: Crafts balanced morning, afternoon, and evening plans with realistic transit times.
* **Local Dining & Event Recommendations**: Highlights seasonal specials, live music, exhibitions, and top-rated venues.
* **Summary Dispatch**: Sends the finalized itinerary directly via SMS or messaging channel.

## Recommended tools & integrations

* **Places & Booking APIs**: Connect Google Places, TripAdvisor, or Yelp for live business hours and ratings.
* **Knowledge Base**: Store local city guides, hotel amenity menus, and partner attraction discounts.
* **Messaging Integrations**: Send formatted itinerary summaries via WhatsApp or SMS.
