# Cart Recovery Specialist

> Re-engages shoppers who abandoned their shopping carts, resolves checkout hesitations, answers product questions, and recovers lost sales.

The **Cart Recovery Specialist** provides a high-touch sales recovery experience for e-commerce stores. It contacts customers shortly after an abandoned session to uncover blockers—such as unexpected shipping costs, payment questions, or product fit concerns—and offers solutions and personalized incentives.

## Key capabilities

* **Checkout Friction Identification**: Identifies why the shopper dropped off (payment failure, shipping cost, uncertainty).
* **Product Clarification & Recommendations**: Answers sizing, compatibility, delivery timelines, and return policies.
* **Incentive Negotiation & Code Generation**: Offers limited-time discount codes or free shipping within approved business rules.
* **Instant Checkout Link Dispatch**: Sends direct recovery links via SMS or WhatsApp during or immediately after the call.

## Recommended tools & integrations

* **Shopify / WooCommerce / Custom E-commerce**: Sync abandoned checkout events and inventory availability.
* **Knowledge Base**: Store return policies, warranty details, and sizing guides.
* **Discounts & Coupons API**: Validate and generate dynamic discount vouchers.
