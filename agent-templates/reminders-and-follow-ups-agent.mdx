# Reminders & Follow-ups Agent

> Automates appointment reminders, overdue payment follow-ups, and scheduled confirmations with natural conversational handling.

The **Reminders & Follow-ups Agent** reduces no-show rates and accelerates collection cycles. It proactively calls contacts before appointments, interviews, or payment deadlines, verifies whether they plan to attend, and can immediately reschedule if their plans have changed.

## Key capabilities

* **Interactive Attendance Confirmation**: Checks if the recipient is attending, needing rescheduling, or requesting cancellation.
* **Instant Rescheduling**: Proposes next available calendar slots and updates booking systems in real-time.
* **Payment Due Notices**: Respectfully informs customers of upcoming or overdue bills and sends payment links via SMS/WhatsApp.
* **Smart Voice Mail & Answering Machine Handling**: Leaves concise voicemail messages or flags for a retry when unreachable.

## Recommended tools & integrations

* **Calendar & Appointment Systems**: Connect Calendly, Acuity, Google Calendar, or custom CRM webhooks.
* **Payment Gateways**: Trigger payment link dispatches when a customer confirms intent to pay.
* **Campaign Manager**: Power bulk automated reminder runs via Sayvy Campaigns.
