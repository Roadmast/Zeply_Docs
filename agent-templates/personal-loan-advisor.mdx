# Personal Loan Advisor

> Guides applicants through personal loan offerings, verifies eligibility criteria, calculates indicative EMIs, and collects application details.

The **Personal Loan Advisor** acts as a reliable financial representative, conducting initial discovery with prospective borrowers. It clearly explains interest rates, tenure options, processing fees, and document requirements while collecting income and employment details to pre-qualify applicants.

## Key capabilities

* **Eligibility Pre-Screening**: Verifies monthly income, employment status, credit score range, and requested loan amount.
* **EMI Calculations**: Provides realistic monthly installment calculations based on tenure and interest ranges.
* **Documentation Guidance**: Outlines required paperwork (ID proof, salary slips, bank statements) to speed up approvals.
* **Lead Qualification & Routing**: Tags high-intent borrowers and connects them directly with senior loan officers.

## Recommended tools & integrations

* **Custom API Tools**: Connect loan origination and credit scoring calculation endpoints.
* **Knowledge Base**: Upload lending policies, rate sheets, fee disclosures, and regulatory guidelines.
* **Webhook Forwarding**: Post pre-qualified borrower records into your CRM or Loan Management System (LMS).
