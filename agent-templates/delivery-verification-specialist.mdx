# Delivery Verification Specialist

> Verifies delivery addresses, confirms Cash on Delivery (COD) readiness, and checks recipient availability to reduce Return to Origin (RTO).

The **Delivery Verification Specialist** prevents failed deliveries and logistics delays by proactively contacting buyers before packages ship or while drivers are out for delivery. It verifies street addresses, nearby landmarks, payment readiness, and preferred delivery windows.

## Key capabilities

* **Address Correction & Landmark Capture**: Validates postal codes, building numbers, and captures delivery instructions.
* **COD Order Confirmation**: Reconfirms customer intent for cash-on-delivery shipments to prevent fake orders.
* **Preferred Time Slot Scheduling**: Notes morning, afternoon, or evening delivery preferences for courier partners.
* **Cancellation & Hold Handling**: Immediately flags cancelled orders to stop fulfillment center dispatch.

## Recommended tools & integrations

* **Order Management System (OMS) / WMS**: Update dispatch tags, addresses, and delivery statuses automatically.
* **Location & Geocoding APIs**: Verify address validity against shipping provider coverage zones.
* **Courier Webhooks**: Trigger automated confirmation calls as soon as an order is placed.
