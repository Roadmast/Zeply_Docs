# News & Updates Caller

> Broadcasts timely voice updates, emergency announcements, service maintenance alerts, and corporate news to customers or staff.

The **News & Updates Caller** delivers high-importance voice broadcasts at scale. Rather than relying on unread emails or overlooked text messages, it places automated voice calls to deliver clear updates, verify receipt, and answer follow-up questions from listeners.

## Key capabilities

* **High-Clarity Voice Announcements**: Delivers pre-scripted or dynamic bulletin updates with professional pacing.
* **Interactive Q&A Handling**: Understands and answers common follow-up questions regarding the news release or event.
* **Acknowledgment Verification**: Confirms that the listener heard and understood critical alerts.
* **Callback & SMS Dispatch**: Dispatches links or full summaries to the recipient's phone upon request.

## Recommended tools & integrations

* **Sayvy Campaigns**: Segment recipient lists and initiate automated voice campaigns.
* **Knowledge Base**: Store press releases, service outage FAQs, and official statements.
* **Notification Webhooks**: Record successful delivery and recipient response timestamps.
