# Lead Qualification Specialist

> Qualifies inbound business leads using BANT framework — evaluates Budget, Authority, Need, and Timeline, and books executive sales demos.

The **Lead Qualification Specialist** acts as an autonomous Sales Development Representative (SDR). It contacts prospects moments after form submissions or handles inbound inquiries, screens them against your Ideal Customer Profile (ICP), and schedules meetings directly onto account executive calendars.

## Key capabilities

* **BANT & ICP Framework Screening**: Evaluates current tech stack, company size, budget allocation, decision-maker status, and purchasing urgency.
* **Smart Objection Handling**: Clarifies feature differentiators, compliance certifications, and pricing structures.
* **Instant Sales Demo Booking**: Hands off qualified prospects directly to AE calendars with meeting links.
* **CRM Lead Enrichment**: Writes detailed discovery summaries and qualification tags to your CRM records.

## Recommended tools & integrations

* **CRM (HubSpot / Salesforce)**: Create contact, company, and deal records with call summaries.
* **Calendar Booking (Google / Outlook / Calendly)**: Book qualified executive meetings instantly.
* **Knowledge Base**: Add product whitepapers, case studies, competitive comparison cards, and pricing models.
