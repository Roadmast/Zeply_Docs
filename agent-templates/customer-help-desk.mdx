## Key capabilities

* **Instant FAQ & Policy Resolution**: Responds accurately to refund queries, login issues, billing dates, and service tiers.
* **Guided Technical Troubleshooting**: Walks users through step-by-step diagnostic workflows.
* **Ticket Creation & CRM Lookup**: Pulls customer account details and logs transcripts into Zendesk, Freshdesk, or Jira.
* **Smart Warm Escalation**: Transfers frustrated or high-priority accounts to live support agents with call summaries.

## Recommended tools & integrations

* **Knowledge Base**: Connect support documentation, help center articles, and release notes.
* **Ticketing Systems**: Integrate with Zendesk, Freshdesk, or internal support webhooks.
* **Call Recording & Transcription**: Access full audio recordings and sentiment analytics in Sayvy Call Logs.
