# AI Recruitment & Interview Specialist

> Conducts first-round voice interviews — screens applicant qualifications, confirms compensation expectations, and schedules hiring manager rounds.

The **AI Recruitment & Interview Specialist** streamlines high-volume talent acquisition. It calls applicants or accepts inbound candidate screenings, assesses background and key requirements, verifies work authorization, and schedules qualified candidates for formal interviews.

## Key capabilities

* **Structured Resume & Experience Screening**: Validates years of relevant experience, core tool proficiencies, and notice periods.
* **Compensation & Logistics Alignment**: Checks salary expectations, remote vs. on-site preference, and visa sponsorship needs.
* **Hiring Manager Calendar Scheduling**: Automatically books formal interview rounds with the interview panel.
* **Candidate Q&A**: Answers candidate questions regarding company culture, team size, interview process, and benefits.

## Recommended tools & integrations

* **Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS)**: Sync with Greenhouse, Lever, Workable, or BambooHR.
* **Calendar Booking**: Direct recruiter and hiring manager schedule coordination.
* **Knowledge Base**: Store job descriptions, benefit summaries, company culture docs, and screening rubrics.
