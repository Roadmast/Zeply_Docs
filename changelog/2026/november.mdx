<Update label="19th November, 2026" description="Voice & WhatsApp">
  ## WhatsApp Outbound Calling & Instant Delivery Receipts

  Agents can now initiate outbound phone calls directly over WhatsApp voice calling, paired with real-time delivery receipt telemetry.

  ### What's New
  * **Outbound WhatsApp Voice Calling:** Trigger outbound voice agent calls directly to verified contacts over WhatsApp voice protocols without requiring standard PSTN telephony trunks.
  * **Campaign Integration:** Include WhatsApp voice calls in batch outbound campaigns alongside WhatsApp text broadcasts.
  * **Real-Time Delivery Status Tracking:** Fixed a latency lag where Meta message delivery receipts (`delivered`, `read`, `failed`) were delayed under heavy webhook volume. Receipts now stream immediately into the inbox and analytics timeline.

<Update label="17th November, 2026" description="Platform & Architecture">
  ## Multi-Tenant Workspaces & Cross-Workspace Isolation

  Organize your voice agents, contacts, integrations, and campaigns into isolated workspaces under a single parent organization.

  ### Key Capabilities
  * **Tenant Data Isolation:** Each workspace functions as an independent environment with its own contact database, knowledge base documents, channels, API keys, and audit logs.
  * **Instant Workspace Switching:** Switch between workspaces via the organization dropdown in the top sidebar without re-authenticating.
  * **Scoped Member Permissions:** Invite team members to specific workspaces (e.g., *Customer Support Workspace* vs. *Outbound Sales Workspace*) with tailored access levels.
  * **Zero-Downtime Migration:** Existing accounts were seamlessly transitioned into a default primary workspace with complete data integrity.

  Empower agents with external data sources and real-time CRM mutations during live voice and text interactions.

  ### Tool Capabilities
  * **Native CRM Operations:** Agents can autonomously update contact lifecycles, adjust lead scores, assign tags, append internal notes, and schedule follow-up tasks during conversations.
  * **Model Context Protocol (MCP) Integration:** Connect any standardized MCP server to supply your agent with custom backend databases, internal APIs, or calendar scheduling tools.

<Update label="3rd November, 2026" description="Developers & Webhooks">
  ## Real-Time Webhooks & Dead Letter Queue (DLQ)

  Subscribe your backend to real-time events across calls, conversations, campaigns, and lead mutations.

  ### Infrastructure Updates
  * **Event Topics:** Subscribe to events including `call.completed`, `message.received`, `lead.created`, and `campaign.finished`.
  * **Dead Letter Queue (DLQ):** Failed webhook dispatches automatically enter a retry loop with exponential backoff, preventing lost telemetry during subscriber outages.
  * **Signature Verification:** Verify webhook authenticity using the `X-Sayvy-Signature` HMAC-SHA256 header.

  [Webhooks Guide](/guides/webhooks) · [Webhooks API Reference](/api-reference/webhooks/overview) · [Dead Letter Queue Architecture](/documentation/workflows/outbound-webhooks-dlq)
</Update>
