When building or asking an AI assistant to generate configurations for Sayvy AI, follow these technical specifications to ensure production reliability.

---

## 1. Voice Agent Prompt Engineering Rules

Voice agents interact over audio streams where speech synthesizers convert text to speech in real time. Standard chatbot prompts will cause poor user experiences on phone calls.

### Spoken Output Constraints
* **No Markdown Formatting:** Never emit asterisks (`**bold**`), hashtags (`# Header`), bullet points (`- Item`), or backticks in spoken output. Text-to-speech engines will either speak the symbols or introduce awkward pauses.
* **Keep Turns Under 2 Sentences:** Spoken dialog must be concise. Limit responses to 1–2 short sentences per turn before returning the floor to the user.
* **Phonetic Clarity:**
  * Spell out complex abbreviations or write them phonetically if TTS mispronounces them (e.g., write "S A P" instead of "SAP" if you want individual letters spoken).
  * Format currency as words: write "one hundred dollars" rather than "$100".
  * For phone numbers, format with dashes or spaces: `4 1 5, 5 5 5, 2 6 7 1`.

## 2. Dynamic Variable Injection

System prompts support dynamic mustache variables injected from contact records or campaign metadata at call initialization:

```text
You are an appointment confirmation assistant calling {{contact.name}}.
Their scheduled appointment is on {{custom_fields.appointment_date}} at {{custom_fields.appointment_time}}.
```

Available default variables:
* `{{contact.name}}`: Customer full name.
* `{{contact.phone_number}}`: E.164 phone number.
* `{{contact.lifecycle_stage}}`: Current CRM lifecycle stage.
* `{{contact.lead_score}}`: Lead score integer.
* `{{custom_fields.<key>}}`: Custom JSON attributes attached to the contact.

## 3. CRM Activity & Multi-Tenancy Rules

When creating CRM records or modifying contact lifecycles:

* **Workspace Scoping:** All activities and contacts require `workspace_id`. Organization ID (`organization_id`) is reserved for billing and organization membership, not contact activity logs.
* **Valid Lifecycle Stages:**
  * `new`: Ingested contact, no human or AI conversation completed yet.
  * `engaged`: Inbound/outbound conversation in progress.
  * `qualified`: Lead criteria met (score > threshold).
  * `customer`: Deal closed / converted.
  * `lost`: Disqualified or unsubscribed.
* **Valid Activity Types:**
  * `contact_created`, `message_inbound`, `message_outbound`, `stage_changed`, `lead_score_changed`, `handoff_to_human`, `handoff_to_ai`, `note_added`, `tag_added`, `task_created`.

## 4. Human Handoff Patterns

When a customer asks to speak with a human agent or becomes frustrated:

1. Acknowledge the request calmly.
2. Trigger the transfer tool configured on the agent, or set `ai_handling` to `false` with the `update_contact_crm` MCP tool.
3. Inform the caller that a human representative is being connected.

```text
If the customer asks to speak with a human or expresses anger, say:
"I completely understand. Let me transfer you to one of our team members right now. Please hold for just a moment."
Then trigger your configured transfer tool.
```
