You'll upload a file so your agents can answer from it, then check that they do. Every agent in the workspace can search every file you upload.

<Visual note="Documents page with a few uploaded files listed" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Documents">
    Open **Resources**, then **Documents**.

    <Visual note="Sidebar with Resources and Documents highlighted" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload a file">
    Click the upload button and choose a PDF, DOCX, or TXT file, such as a price list or FAQ. Give it a clear name.

    <Visual kind="gif" note="Choosing a file and watching it upload" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait until it's ready">
    Once the upload finishes the document is searchable.

    <Visual note="The uploaded document listed as ready" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask a question from it">
    Click **Agents**, open an agent, click **Test Agent**, and ask something only the file can answer.

    <Visual note="Test chat answering a price question from the file" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove old versions">
    When a file is out of date, delete it from **Documents**. Agents stop finding it on their next search.

    <Visual note="Delete action on a document row" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Don't upload secrets or personal data your agents don't need.
</Warning>
