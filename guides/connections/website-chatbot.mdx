You'll switch the widget on for an agent, style it, and paste one script into your site.

<Visual kind="gif" note="From the Deploy tab to the widget opening on a live page" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the agent's Deploy tab">
    Click **Agents**, open your agent, and click the **Deploy** tab.

    <Visual note="Deploy tab of an agent" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch on the widget">
    Turn on **Web Chat Widget**. Turn on **Enable Voice** too if visitors should be able to call.

    <Visual note="Web Chat Widget and Enable Voice switched on" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Configure Embed Script">
    The widget settings open on the **Connections** page with a live preview beside them. Change the title, greeting, suggested questions, and colours.

    <Visual note="Widget settings with the live preview" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the script">
    Copy the embed script shown on the page.

    <Visual note="Embed script with the copy button" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste it into your site">
    Paste it in your site's HTML, just before the closing `</head>` tag.

    <Visual note="Script pasted in a site's head section" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try it as a visitor">
    Open your site, click the launcher button in the corner, and send a message.

    <Visual kind="gif" note="Opening the widget on a live page and chatting with the agent" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Widget chats show up in **Inbox**, but browser calls aren't saved to call logs.
</Note>
