You'll sign in with Meta and bring your WhatsApp Business number into Sayvy AI. You need a Meta business account with a number registered for the WhatsApp Business Platform.

<Visual kind="gif" note="Connect WhatsApp from the card, through Meta sign-in, to the connected card" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Connections">
    Click **Connections** in the sidebar and find the **WhatsApp Business** card.

    <Visual note="WhatsApp Business card before connecting" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect WhatsApp">
    You're taken to Meta. Sign in with an account that has admin access to your business.

    <Visual note="Meta sign-in screen" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve access">
    Choose your business and number, and approve. Meta sends you back to Sayvy AI.

    <Visual note="Meta business and number selection screen" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="See the connected card">
    A message confirms the connection. The card now shows your phone number and the date.

    <Visual note="Connected WhatsApp card with phone number and date" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a test message">
    From your phone, message the number. The conversation appears in **Inbox**.

    <Visual note="Inbox showing the test conversation" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
If you see "Failed to connect WhatsApp Business account", try again and check you signed in as a business admin. Access expires after about 60 days; click **Reconnect WhatsApp** when that happens.
</Note>
