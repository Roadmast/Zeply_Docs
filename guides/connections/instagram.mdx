You'll link an Instagram professional account so its direct messages arrive in the inbox.

<Visual kind="gif" note="Connect Instagram from the card to the first message in the inbox" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Connections">
    Click **Connections** in the sidebar and find the **Instagram Direct** card.

    <Visual note="Instagram Direct card before connecting" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect Instagram">
    Sign in with your Instagram professional account and approve access.

    <Visual note="Instagram sign-in and permission screen" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="See the connected card">
    You return to **Connections** and the card shows the connected account.

    <Visual note="Connected Instagram card" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a test message">
    From another profile, send a direct message to the account. Click **Inbox** and open the new conversation.

    <Visual note="Inbox with an Instagram conversation open" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
AI agents don't reply on Instagram yet, so messages wait for your team.
</Note>
