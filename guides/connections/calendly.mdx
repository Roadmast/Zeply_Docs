You'll connect Calendly so an agent can send customers your scheduling link.

<Visual kind="gif" note="Connect Calendly and the agent sending the link in a test chat" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Connections">
    Click **Connections** in the sidebar and find the **Calendly** card.

    <Visual note="Calendly card before connecting" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect Calendly">
    Sign in with your Calendly account.

    <Visual note="Calendly sign-in screen" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="See the connected card">
    You return to **Connections**. The card shows when you connected and when the sign-in expires.

    <Visual note="Connected Calendly card" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn booking on for an agent">
    Open the agent, click **Tools & Integrations**, and turn on **Enable Booking**. In the booking instructions, tell the agent to send the scheduling link.

    <Visual note="Booking instructions telling the agent to send the link" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try it">
    Click **Test Agent**, ask to book an appointment, and look for your link in the reply.

    <Visual note="Test chat with the Calendly link in the agent's reply" />
  </Step>
</Steps>
