title: "Connect Google Calendar"

---

import { Visual } from "/snippets/visual.mdx";

You'll sign in with the Google account whose primary calendar should hold your appointments, then turn booking on for an agent.

<Visual kind="gif" note="Connect Calendar, Google consent, and the connected card" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Connections">
    Click **Connections** in the sidebar and find the **Google Calendar** card.

    <Visual note="Google Calendar card before connecting" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Connect Calendar">
    Sign in with Google and approve the access it asks for.

    <Visual note="Google consent screen" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="See the connected card">
    You return to **Connections**. The card shows when you connected and when the sign-in expires.

    <Visual note="Connected Google Calendar card" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn booking on for an agent">
    Open the agent, click **Tools & Integrations**, and turn on **Enable Booking**.

    <Visual note="Tools & Integrations tab with Enable Booking on" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Book a test slot">
    Click **Test Agent**, ask for an appointment, then check your Google Calendar for the event.

    <Visual kind="gif" note="Test conversation ending with an event in Google Calendar" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
The **Real-time Booking** row always says "Verified". It's a label, so confirm with a test booking.
</Note>
