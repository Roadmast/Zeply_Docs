Email arrives by forwarding. You'll get a forwarding address from Sayvy AI and point your mailbox at it.

<Visual kind="gif" note="Connect Email through forwarding to the first email in the inbox" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Connect Email">
    Open **Connections**, find the **Email Channel** card, and click **Connect Email**.

    <Visual note="Email Channel card with Connect Email highlighted" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your address">
    Type the address customers write to and continue.

    <Visual note="Address entry dialog" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the forwarding address">
    Sayvy AI shows an address to forward to. Copy it.

    <Visual note="Forwarding address shown by Sayvy AI" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a forwarding rule in your mail app">
    In Gmail or Outlook, add auto-forwarding to that address.

    <Visual note="Gmail forwarding settings with the address pasted in" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm forwarding">
    Your mail provider sends a confirmation message. Sayvy AI catches it and shows the link or code on screen. Approve it there.

    <Visual note="Confirmation link or code shown in Sayvy AI" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a test email">
    Write to your address from another account. It appears in **Inbox**.

    <Visual note="Inbox showing the test email" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
AI agents don't reply on email yet; your team answers from the inbox.
</Note>
