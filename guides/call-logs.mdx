**Call Logs** lists every WhatsApp voice call your agents handle, in both directions. You'll find a call and open its recording and transcript.

<Visual note="Call Logs page with a list of calls" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Call Logs">
    Click **Call Logs** in the left sidebar. Each call shows its direction, numbers, status, duration, and time.

    <Visual note="Call Logs list in card view" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch the view">
    Use the view toggle to move between cards and a table.

    <Visual note="View toggle set to table" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Narrow the list">
    Search by call ID or phone number, or filter by direction (inbound or outbound) and status.

    <Visual note="Filters set to inbound and completed" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a call">
    Click **Details** on a call. You see the overview, a **Voice Recording** player, a summary, and the **Call Transcription**.

    <Visual note="Call details with the recording player and transcript" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the audio">
    Click **Download Audio File** under **Voice Recording**.

    <Visual note="Download Audio File button" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Recordings and transcripts exist only if the agent had them turned on at the time of the call. Browser calls from the web widget aren't logged. See [Set recording and follow-ups](/guides/agents/advanced-settings).
</Note>
