**CRM Hub** is where every contact lives. Most are created automatically when someone first reaches you. This walkthrough finds your way around it.

<Visual note="CRM Hub with the contacts table and stage cards" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open CRM Hub">
    Click **CRM Hub** in the left sidebar. The table lists contacts with name, phone, stage, lead score, handling, and tags, most recently active first.

    <Visual note="Contacts table" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter the list">
    Use the search box, the **Handling** filter, the **Score** filter, or click a stage card at the top.

    <Visual note="Filters applied and a stage card selected" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch views">
    Switch between the table, the **Kanban** view, and the **Tasks** view.

    <Visual note="View switcher with Kanban, table, and Tasks" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a contact">
    Click **View Profile** on a row.

    <Visual note="Contact profile open" />
  </Step>
</Steps>
