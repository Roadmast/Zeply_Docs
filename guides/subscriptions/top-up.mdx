You'll add credits to your balance. Do it before a large campaign, because calls stop when credits run out.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing">
    Click **Billing** in the sidebar, or click the balance in the sidebar.

    <Visual note="Sidebar balance being clicked" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Top-Up Credits">
    Scroll to **Top-Up Credits**.

    <Visual note="Top-Up Credits section" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an amount">
    Drag the slider or click a quick pick. The minimum is 500 credits and the maximum is 25,000 per top-up.

    <Visual kind="gif" note="Dragging the slider and the order summary updating" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the order summary">
    It shows the credit value, any discount, GST, and the total.

    <Visual note="Order summary with a volume discount" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Buy Credits">
    Pay in Razorpay's checkout. The new balance appears in the sidebar.

    <Visual note="Updated balance in the sidebar" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Credits cost ₹1 each plus 18% GST and never expire. The dashboard doesn't warn you when the balance runs low, so check the sidebar.
</Note>
