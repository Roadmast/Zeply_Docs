Quick replies are saved messages your team can drop into any conversation. You'll create one and use it in the inbox.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Quick Replies">
    Go to **Message Library**, then **Quick Replies**.

    <Visual note="Message Library with Quick Replies selected" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click New Quick Reply">
    A form opens.

    <Visual note="New Quick Reply form, empty" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill it in">
    Enter a **Shortcut** such as `/hours`, and the **Message Content**:

    ```text
    We're open 9am to 7pm, Monday to Saturday. On Sundays
    we open from 10am to 2pm.
    ```

    <Visual note="Form filled in with /hours" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The quick reply is shared with everyone in the workspace.

    <Visual note="Quick replies list with the new entry" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use it in a conversation">
    In **Inbox**, open a conversation and type `/`. Keep typing to narrow the list, pick one with the arrow keys, and press Enter. Edit the text before you send.

    <Visual kind="gif" note="Typing /hours in the composer and the reply filling in" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Quick replies can't be edited. To change one, delete it and create it again with the same shortcut. They are text only; attach files from the composer.
</Note>
