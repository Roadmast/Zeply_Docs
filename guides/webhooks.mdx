A webhook sends an HTTP POST to your server when something happens, such as a message arriving or a call ending. You'll add an endpoint.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Webhooks">
    Click **Developers**, then **Webhooks**.

    <Visual note="Developers menu with Webhooks selected" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Endpoint">
    A form opens.

    <Visual note="Add Endpoint form, empty" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the Payload URL">
    Type your server's HTTPS address.

    <Visual note="Payload URL field filled in" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose events">
    Under **Event Subscriptions**, tick only the events you need, such as `message.received` or `call.completed`.

    <Visual note="Event Subscriptions with two events ticked" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and copy the secret">
    Save the endpoint. The signing secret, starting with `whsec_`, is shown once. Copy it now.

    <Visual note="Signing secret shown once with the copy button" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it">
    There's no test button. Subscribe to `message.received` and message your WhatsApp number to send a real event.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
To change events or turn an endpoint off, choose **Edit Endpoint** and use **Endpoint Status**. Endpoints per plan: none on Pay as you go, 1 on Basic, 3 on Advanced, 10 on Premium.
</Note>
