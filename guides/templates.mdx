A template is a WhatsApp message Meta has approved in advance. You need one to start a conversation, to message someone after 24 hours of silence, and to run a campaign. They live on the **Message Templates** page.

<Visual note="Message Templates page with a list of templates and their status column" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Message Templates">
    Click **Message Templates** in the left sidebar.

    <Visual note="Sidebar with Message Templates highlighted" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the status column">
    Only approved templates can be sent. Pending ones are still in Meta's review, and rejected ones need a rewrite.

    <Visual note="Status column showing approved, pending, and rejected" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add or bring in a template">
    Click **+ Create Template** to build one, or **Sync from Meta** to pull in ones you made elsewhere.

    <Visual note="Create Template and Sync from Meta buttons" />
  </Step>
</Steps>
