# Manage your subscription

Workspace billing admins can review the current plan and available subscription actions from **Billing → Subscription**.

## Before you change plans

Review current usage, confirm the workspace you are changing, and tell affected teammates when limits or features will change. After the change, verify the new status and check the next invoice.

For billing records, see [Invoices](/guides/invoices). For a broader overview, see [Billing](/guides/billing).
