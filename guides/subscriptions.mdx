A subscription unlocks features and sets your limits. Credits pay for voice minutes and AI replies. You'll open the billing page and subscribe to a plan.

<Visual note="Billing page with the credit balance and Subscription Plans" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing">
    Click **Billing** in the left sidebar. Your credit balance shows at the top.

    <Visual note="Sidebar with Billing highlighted and the balance visible" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Subscription Plans">
    Switch between **Monthly** and **Yearly**. Yearly billing saves 20%.

    <Visual note="Monthly and Yearly switch above the plan cards" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Subscribe">
    Click **Subscribe** on the plan you want. Razorpay's checkout opens.

    <Visual note="Plan cards with Subscribe highlighted" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    Complete the payment in the checkout. You return to the billing page with the new plan active.

    <Visual note="Razorpay checkout screen" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Buying a new plan ends the old one straight away, with no proration, so change plans at the start of a billing cycle. The dashboard has no cancel button yet.
</Warning>
