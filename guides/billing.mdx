# Manage billing

Billing is organized around your workspace subscription and usage. Workspace admins can review the current plan, payment details, and billing history.

  <Card title="Subscriptions" icon="credit-card" href="/guides/subscriptions">Change the plan and review subscription status.</Card>
  <Card title="Invoices" icon="file-text" href="/guides/invoices">Open and download past invoices.</Card>

## Before changing a plan

Check which workspace you are viewing, review current usage, and confirm who owns billing access. Plan changes can affect the limits and features available to every member.
