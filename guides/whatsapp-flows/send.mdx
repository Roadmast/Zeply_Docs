You'll send a published Flow to a contact and read what they submit.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Flow">
    On **WhatsApp Flows**, click a published Flow.

    <Visual note="Flows list with a published Flow" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the send option">
    Enter the contact's WhatsApp number. Add an optional header, message body, footer, and button text.

    <Visual note="Send Flow form filled in" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Click send. The customer sees a button that opens the form inside the chat.

    <Visual kind="gif" note="Customer opening and filling in the Flow on a phone" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the submissions">
    Open the Flow and look at its submissions, or open the global submissions list to see every Flow at once.

    <Visual note="Submissions table with one entry" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
A Flow sent on its own needs an open 24-hour window. To reach many contacts, add a **Flow Button** to a template and send it as a campaign.
</Warning>
