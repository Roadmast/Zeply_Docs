You'll build a lead capture form and publish it. Only published Flows can be sent.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click New Flow">
    On **WhatsApp Flows**, click **New Flow**.

    <Visual note="WhatsApp Flows page with New Flow highlighted" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Enter a name, such as "Lead Capture Form", and choose a category.

    <Visual note="New Flow dialog filled in" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a screen">
    In the editor, add a screen.

    <Visual note="Editor with one screen" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add fields">
    On the screen, add the form fields you need, such as name and phone number.

    <Visual kind="gif" note="Adding two fields to a screen" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview">
    Click **Preview** to see how it looks on a phone.

    <Visual note="Phone preview of the Flow" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    When it's ready, publish it. The status in the list changes to published.

    <Visual note="Flow list with the new Flow published" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Built a Flow elsewhere? Click **Import Flow JSON** to paste it in.
</Note>
