import { Visual } from "/snippets/visual.mdx";

You'll find a payment and download it as a PDF.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Invoices">
    Click **Billing** in the sidebar, then **Invoices**.

    <Visual note="Billing page with Invoices selected" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a tab">
    Payments are split into **Plans**, **Add-ons**, and **AI Credits**.

    <Visual note="The three tabs with AI Credits selected" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download it">
    Find the payment and download it as a PDF.

    <Visual note="Invoice row with the download button" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
These are payment receipts. They aren't GST tax invoices and don't include a GSTIN.
</Note>
