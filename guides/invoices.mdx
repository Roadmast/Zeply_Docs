# View invoices

Workspace billing admins can use **Billing → Invoices** to review past charges and download invoices for finance or record keeping.

## Find the right invoice

Check the workspace name, billing period, invoice status, and amount before downloading. If an invoice does not match the subscription or usage you expected, review [Subscriptions](/guides/subscriptions) and contact the billing owner.

<Note>Keep invoice access limited to the people who need it. Invoices may include workspace and payment details.</Note>
