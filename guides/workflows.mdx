A workflow agent follows a conversation you draw as a graph. This walkthrough opens the builder and starts a draft.

<Visual note="Workflow Builder with a small graph on the canvas" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click New Agent">
    Click **Agents**, then **New Agent**, and choose **Workflow Agent**.

    <Visual note="New Agent dialog with Workflow Agent highlighted" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Land in the builder">
    The Workflow Builder opens with three tabs: **Editor**, **Test**, and **Executions**. You start on the **Editor** canvas.

    <Visual note="Builder header with the three tabs" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start from a template">
    Pick a template, such as **Lead qualification** or **Appointment request**. Or type what you want and click **Build with AI** to draft a graph.

    <Visual note="Template picker with five templates" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the draft">
    The graph appears on the canvas. Your work saves automatically; the header shows when it last saved.

    <Visual note="Canvas with a template loaded and the saved-at time in the header" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
**Build with AI** uses AI credits and opens as a draft for you to review.
</Note>
