A campaign sends one WhatsApp template to a group of contacts. This walkthrough shows where campaigns live and the route from idea to results.

<Visual note="Campaigns page with a list of campaigns and the status tabs" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Campaigns">
    Click **Campaigns** in the left sidebar. Each campaign shows its sent, delivered, read, and failed counts.

    <Visual note="Campaign list with the four counts" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create one">
    Click **New Campaign**. See [Create a template campaign](/guides/campaigns/create).

    <Visual note="New Campaign button" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Control it">
    Pause, resume, or cancel from the campaign. See [Pause, resume, and cancel](/guides/campaigns/manage).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the results">
    Open a campaign for its delivery funnel. See [Read campaign results](/guides/campaigns/results).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Template campaigns aren't available on the Pay as you go plan.
</Warning>
