**Analytics** shows how your workspace is doing over a time range. You'll open it and look at each tab.

<Visual note="Analytics page, Overview tab, with charts" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Analytics">
    Click **Analytics** in the left sidebar.

    <Visual note="Sidebar with Analytics highlighted" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a time range">
    Choose the last 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days, or 90 days.

    <Visual note="Time range selector" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start with Overview">
    The **Overview** tab shows total calls, messages, call time, and cost, with traffic trends and a channel breakdown.

    <Visual note="Overview tab" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Voice Calls">
    Open **Voice Calls** for success rate, failed and missed calls, and the busiest hours of the day.

    <Visual note="Voice Calls tab with the busiest-hours heatmap" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare the rest">
    **Messaging & Inbox** shows delivery and read rates. **Campaigns** shows what you sent. **Agent Performance** compares your agents.

    <Visual note="Agent Performance tab with a table of agents" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Your credit balance is in the sidebar. Click it to open **Billing** and top up.
</Note>
