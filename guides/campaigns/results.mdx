You'll see how a campaign performed and download a report to share.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the list">
    On **Campaigns**, each row shows sent, delivered, read, and failed counts. Use the tabs above the list to filter by status.

    <Visual note="Campaign list with the four counts and status tabs" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a campaign">
    Click the campaign to see its status, audience, launch time, and the delivery funnel.

    <Visual note="Delivery funnel with sent, delivered, read, failed" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the funnel">
    A big drop from sent to delivered often means numbers that aren't on WhatsApp. Few reads among delivered messages often means the opening line isn't working.

    <Visual note="Funnel with a large sent-to-delivered gap" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the report">
    Click the download button for a PDF with the counts and rates.

    <Visual note="PDF report download button and the report" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Contacts with read receipts off never show as read. The dashboard funnel and the PDF cover messages only, not calls.
</Note>
