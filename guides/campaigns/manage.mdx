title: "Pause, resume, and cancel"
description: "Control a campaign after it launches."
---

import { Visual } from "/snippets/visual.mdx";

You'll stop a campaign, restart it, or end it. Open **Campaigns** first.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the campaign">
    Click **Campaigns** in the sidebar, then click the campaign.

    <Visual note="Campaign details page" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pause it">
    Click **Pause**. Sending stops before the next contact. Messages already sent stay sent.

    <Visual note="Pause button and the Paused status" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resume it">
    Click **Resume**. The campaign carries on where it stopped, and nobody gets the message twice.

    <Visual note="Resume button" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cancel it">
    Click **Cancel** and confirm. Messages not yet sent are dropped.

    <Visual note="Cancel confirmation dialog" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Cancelling can't be undone, and a scheduled campaign can't be edited. To fix a typo, pause, check how many went out, cancel, and create a corrected campaign for the rest.
</Warning>
