You'll pick who receives the campaign in the **Audience** step of the builder.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reach the Audience step">
    In the campaign builder, continue past **Basics** and **Template**.

    <Visual note="Builder with the Audience step active" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a strategy">
    Under **Target Audience Strategy**, choose **All Contacts**, **Contacts by Tags**, or **Select Contacts Manually**.

    <Visual note="The three strategy options" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick tags or contacts">
    For tags, tick the tags you want. For manual, search and pick the contacts.

    <Visual note="Tag list with two tags ticked" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the preview">
    The **Eligible Audience Preview** lists the first 20 contacts who will get the message.

    <Visual note="Eligible Audience Preview" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Tags match on any, not all, and opted-out contacts aren't filtered. Tag anyone who asks to stop, for example `do-not-contact`, and target by tags that leave them out.
</Warning>

## Add contacts first

Need more contacts? [Import them from a spreadsheet](/guides/contacts/import) before you build the campaign.
