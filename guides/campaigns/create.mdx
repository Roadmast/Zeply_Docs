You'll send one approved WhatsApp template to a group of contacts. You need a template and a connected WhatsApp number.

<Visual kind="gif" note="The six builder steps from Basics to Launch Campaign" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click New Campaign">
    On **Campaigns**, click **New Campaign**.

    <Visual note="Campaigns page with New Campaign highlighted" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Basics">
    Enter a **Campaign Name** and choose the **WhatsApp Business Channel**, the number messages come from.

    <Visual note="Basics step filled in" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Template">
    Under **Select WhatsApp Template**, pick a template. The preview shows it as contacts will see it.

    <Visual note="Template step with the preview" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Audience">
    Choose who gets it. See [Choose a campaign audience](/guides/campaigns/audience).

    <Visual note="Audience step with Eligible Audience Preview" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Variables">
    This step appears only if the template has placeholders such as `{{1}}`. For each one, choose **Contact Field**, **Same for Everyone**, or **From CSV File**. Set **If missing** to **Use fallback** or **Skip contact**.

    <Visual note="Variables step with a placeholder mapped to a contact field" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule">
    Choose **Send Now (Immediate Launch)** or **Schedule for Later** and pick a time.

    <Visual note="Schedule step" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and launch">
    Check the summary and the live preview, then click **Launch Campaign**.

    <Visual note="Review step with Launch Campaign highlighted" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Only templates Meta has approved are delivered. Templates with an image, video, or document header can't be sent with their media from a campaign yet.
</Note>
