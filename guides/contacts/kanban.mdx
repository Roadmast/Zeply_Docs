The Kanban view lays contacts out in columns by lifecycle stage, so you can see at a glance how many sit at each step.

<Visual note="Kanban view with five stage columns and contact cards" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open CRM Hub">
    Click **CRM Hub** in the left sidebar.

    <Visual note="CRM Hub table view" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch to Kanban">
    Use the view switcher to choose **Kanban**.

    <Visual note="View switcher with Kanban selected" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the columns">
    The columns are the five stages: new, engaged, qualified, customer, and lost. Each card is one contact.

    <Visual note="Columns labelled with the five stages" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a card">
    Click a card to open the contact's profile and change their stage.

    <Visual note="A contact card being opened" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
The stages are fixed. You can't add, rename, or remove them, and the CRM has no deals or multiple pipelines. Use tags or custom fields for anything else.
</Note>
