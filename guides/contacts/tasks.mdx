You'll add a follow-up to a contact, then track it to done.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the contact">
    In **CRM Hub**, click **View Profile** on the contact's row.

    <Visual note="Contact profile" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Task">
    Scroll to **Tasks & Reminders** and click **Add Task**.

    <Visual note="Tasks & Reminders section with Add Task highlighted" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill it in">
    Say what needs doing, add details if you want, and set a due date. A task without one is easy to forget.

    <Visual note="Add Task form filled in" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="See all tasks">
    Back in **CRM Hub**, switch to the **Tasks** view. Use **Pending**, **Completed**, or **All Tasks**, or search by task title or contact name.

    <Visual note="Tasks view on the Pending tab" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark it done">
    Mark the task done from the list or from the contact's profile.

    <Visual note="Task marked as completed" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Tasks aren't available on the Pay as you go plan. Sayvy AI doesn't send reminders when a task is due, so check the **Tasks** view daily.
</Note>
