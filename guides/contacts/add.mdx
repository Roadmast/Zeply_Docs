You'll create a contact yourself, for someone who hasn't messaged you yet.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open CRM Hub">
    Click **CRM Hub** in the left sidebar.

    <Visual note="CRM Hub with New Contact highlighted" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click New Contact">
    A form opens.

    <Visual note="New Contact form, empty" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill it in">
    Enter a **Full Name** and a **WhatsApp Phone Number** with the country code, such as `+919876543210`. Add any **Tags**.

    <Visual note="New Contact form filled in" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click save. The contact appears at the top of the table.

    <Visual note="The new contact in the table" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the rest">
    Click **View Profile** to set notes, stage, and custom fields, which can be set only after the contact is saved.

    <Visual note="Profile of the new contact" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
A phone number can belong to only one contact in the workspace. If it's already taken, the new contact isn't created.
</Note>
