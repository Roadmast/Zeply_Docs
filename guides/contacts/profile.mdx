You'll open a contact and update what your team knows about them.

<Visual note="Contact profile with all sections visible" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the profile">
    In **CRM Hub**, click **View Profile** on the contact's row.

    <Visual note="Row with View Profile highlighted" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the stage and score">
    Change **Lifecycle Stage** and **Lead Score** (0 to 100).

    <Visual note="Lifecycle Stage and Lead Score controls" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who handles them">
    Switch **Conversation Handling** between AI and a human.

    <Visual note="Conversation Handling switch" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit tags and details">
    Edit the tags, or click **Edit Contact** to change the name and phone.

    <Visual note="Tags editor" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a note">
    Open **Activity Timeline**, type in the box at the top, and click **Add Note**.

    <Visual note="Activity Timeline with a note being added" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Jump to the conversation">
    Click **Chat in Inbox** to open their conversation.

    <Visual note="Chat in Inbox button" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
**Delete Contact** removes the contact for good, with their history, tasks, and conversations. To stop messaging someone, tag them instead.
</Warning>
