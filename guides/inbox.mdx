The inbox shows every conversation from WhatsApp, Instagram, email, and your web widget. This walkthrough finds a conversation and opens it.

<Visual note="Inbox with the conversation list on the left and a conversation open" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Inbox">
    Click **Inbox** in the left sidebar. New messages appear on their own, without a refresh.

    <Visual note="Sidebar with Inbox highlighted" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a tab">
    Use **All**, **Open**, or **Closed** at the top of the list to filter the tickets.

    <Visual note="The three tabs with Open selected" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search">
    Type a contact name or phone number in the search box.

    <Visual note="Search box with a name typed in" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a conversation">
    Click a row. The messages open on the right, and the header shows **AI On** or **AI Off**, the close button, and contact details.

    <Visual note="Conversation header with its three controls labelled" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="See who you're talking to">
    Click contact details to open the customer profile beside the conversation.

    <Visual note="Customer profile panel beside a conversation" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
When AI is on, the agent answers text messages on WhatsApp and the web widget. Instagram and email wait for your team.
</Note>
