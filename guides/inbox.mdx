# Work in the inbox

The inbox is the team's operational view of customer conversations. Use it to see what needs attention, assign ownership, and preserve context when a person takes over from an agent.

## Resolve a conversation

1. Filter for new, assigned, or waiting conversations.
2. Read the latest messages and customer context before replying.
3. Reply directly or take ownership when the conversation needs a person.
4. Use a quick reply for a repeatable answer, then personalize it.
5. Update the status when the next action is clear.

<Note>A conversation should always have a visible next step: reply, wait for the customer, hand off, or close.</Note>
