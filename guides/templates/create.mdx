You'll build a template in the builder and send it to Meta for approval.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Create Template">
    On **Message Templates**, click **+ Create Template**. The builder opens with a live preview.

    <Visual note="Template builder with the preview on the right" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Use lowercase letters, numbers, and underscores, such as `order_ready`. Choose the language and the category: Marketing, Utility, or Authentication.

    <Visual note="Name, language, and category fields" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the body">
    Drag in a body and type your message. Use `{{1}}`, `{{2}}` for values that change per contact:

    ```text
    Hi {{1}}, your order {{2}} is ready for pickup at our Baner store.
    Reply here if you'd like it delivered instead.
    ```

    <Visual kind="gif" note="Typing the body and watching the preview update" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the optional parts">
    Add a header, footer, or buttons if you need them. Buttons can be quick reply, URL, phone number, or a published WhatsApp Flow.

    <Visual note="Builder with a footer and two buttons added" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit to Meta">
    Click **Save & Submit to Meta**. The template shows as pending, then changes to approved or rejected. Reviews often take minutes, but can take longer.

    <Visual note="Template list showing the new template as pending" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Meta checks that the category fits the content. If a template is rejected, rewrite it and submit again.
</Note>
