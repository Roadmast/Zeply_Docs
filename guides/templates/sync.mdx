Connecting WhatsApp doesn't bring in your templates. You'll pull them in, or start from Meta's ready-made library.

## Sync your templates

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Message Templates">
    Click **Message Templates** in the sidebar.

    <Visual note="Message Templates page with Sync from Meta highlighted" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Sync from Meta">
    Choose the WhatsApp channel to sync.

    <Visual note="Channel picker in the sync dialog" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the list">
    Every template on that WhatsApp account is pulled in or updated.

    <Visual note="Template list after sync" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Start from Meta's library

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Explore Templates">
    Browse the library. Search or filter by industry.

    <Visual note="Explore Templates with an industry filter" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to add it">
    **Add to Meta** submits the template as it is. **Customize in Builder** opens it so you can change it first.

    <Visual note="A library template with both buttons" />
  </Step>
</Steps>
