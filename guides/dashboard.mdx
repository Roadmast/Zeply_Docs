# Start with the dashboard

The dashboard is your workspace home. Use it to see what is happening across conversations, agents, campaigns, and connected channels, then jump directly into the area that needs your attention.

## A simple first-run path

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm your workspace">Check the workspace name and members in **Settings**.</Step>
  <Step title="Connect a channel">Open **Connections** and connect the channel where your customers already talk to you.</Step>
  <Step title="Create an agent">Add instructions and tools, then test a conversation before making it available to customers.</Step>
  <Step title="Review the first conversation">Use **Inbox** for active conversations and **Call Logs** for completed voice calls.</Step>
</Steps>
