The dashboard is the first screen after you log in. This walkthrough shows where things are and what to set up first.

<Visual note="Full dashboard with the sidebar expanded and the workspace name at the top" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your workspace">
    Look at the workspace name at the top of the left sidebar. Click it to see the workspaces you can switch to.

    <Visual note="Workspace menu open, current workspace highlighted" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect a channel">
    Click **Connections** in the sidebar. You land on a page with one card per service. Click the connect button on the card you want.

    <Visual note="Connections page with the channel cards" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create an agent">
    Click **Agents**, then **New Agent**. The editor opens on the **Prompts & Identity** tab.

    <Visual note="Agents page with New Agent highlighted" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the first conversation">
    Click **Inbox** to see live conversations. Click **Call Logs** to see finished voice calls.

    <Visual note="Inbox with one open conversation" />
  </Step>
</Steps>
