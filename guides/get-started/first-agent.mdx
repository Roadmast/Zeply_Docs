You'll create a voice agent for a dental clinic and talk to it from your browser. It takes about ten minutes.

<Visual kind="gif" note="The whole flow sped up, from New Agent to the first test call" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Agents">
    Click **Agents** in the left sidebar. You land on the list of your agents, which is empty for now.

    <Visual note="Agents page, empty state, sidebar item highlighted" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click New Agent">
    Click **New Agent** at the top right, then choose **Prompt-based AI Agent**.

    <Visual note="New Agent dialog with Prompt-based AI Agent highlighted" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the agent and add a welcome message">
    You're on the **Prompts & Identity** tab. Type a name in **Agent Name**, then paste this into **Welcome Message**:

    ```text
    Hi, you've reached Green Leaf Dental. How can I help?
    ```

    <Visual note="Prompts & Identity tab with name and welcome message filled in" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the prompt">
    Scroll to **Agent prompt** and paste:

    ```text
    You are the front desk for Green Leaf Dental. Answer questions about
    opening hours, prices, and treatments. If someone wants an appointment,
    collect their name and preferred day. Keep answers short. You are on a call.
    ```

    <Visual note="Agent prompt field filled in" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a voice">
    Open the **AI Model & Voice** tab. Switch on **Enable voice**, click a voice in the list, and press play to hear it.

    <Visual note="AI Model & Voice tab, Enable voice on, one voice selected" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Changes** at the top right.

    <Visual note="Save Changes button and the confirmation message" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Talk to it">
    Click **Test Agent**, open the **Webcall** tab, and allow the microphone. Say "What are your opening hours?" The agent answers out loud.

    <Visual kind="gif" note="Test Agent panel, Webcall tab, a short exchange" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

That's a working agent. To go further, see the [full agent editor](/documentation/agents/overview) in the documentation.
