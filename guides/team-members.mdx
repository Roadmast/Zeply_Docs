Only admins can invite people, change roles, or remove members. This walkthrough shows the roster and how to change someone's role or remove them.

<Visual note="Team Management page with the Workspace Roster" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Team Management">
    Click **Settings**, then **Team Management**.

    <Visual note="Settings with Team Management selected" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the roster">
    The **Workspace Roster** lists everyone with their role, status, and when they joined or were invited.

    <Visual note="Roster with roles and statuses" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change a role">
    Click **Role** on the person's row and pick **Admin**, **Member**, or **Viewer**.

    <Visual note="Role menu open on a row" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove someone">
    Click **Remove** on their row and confirm. Do this as soon as someone leaves the team.

    <Visual note="Remove confirmation" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
An organization always needs one admin, so the last admin can't be removed or demoted.
</Note>
