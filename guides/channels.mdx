# Connect communication channels

Channels determine where customers reach your agents and where your team follows up. Keep connection setup separate from agent design so each can be tested independently.

## Before you go live

Verify credentials, send a test message or call, check the agent's identity and tone, and confirm that failures reach the right teammate.
