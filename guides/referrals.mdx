Every organization has a referral code and link. When someone signs up through your link and pays for their first subscription, you both get a discount.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Referrals">
    Open **Referrals** under **Settings**.

    <Visual note="Referrals page with the code and link" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy your link">
    Copy your referral link, or your code, and share it.

    <Visual note="Referral link with the copy button" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your friend signs up">
    They sign up through the link and get 25% off their first subscription payment.

    <Visual note="Signup page opened from a referral link" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track progress">
    The page shows referrals pending and completed, your current tier, and any reward waiting to be applied.

    <Visual note="Referral stats and current tier" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your reward tiers

| Completed referrals | Your discount |
|---|---|
| 1 | 25% |
| 5 | 50% |
| 10 | 75% |

<Note>
A referral completes when the referred organization makes its first paid subscription payment. Discounts don't stack: a higher tier replaces the lower one, and each reward applies to one billing cycle.
</Note>
