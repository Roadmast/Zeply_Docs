You'll invite a teammate by email. You need to be an admin.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Team Management">
    Click **Settings**, then **Team Management**.

    <Visual note="Team Management page" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Invite Member">
    A form opens.

    <Visual note="Invite Member form, empty" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill it in">
    Enter their **Full Name** and **Business Email**, and choose a role: **Admin**, **Member**, or **Viewer**.

    <Visual note="Invite form filled in" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invite">
    Send it. They get an email with a link that works for 7 days. Opening it lets them set a password and join straight away.

    <Visual note="Roster showing the invite as pending" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Handle an expired link">
    If the link expired before they used it, click **Reinvite** on their row. Click **Cancel** to withdraw an invite nobody accepted.

    <Visual note="Pending row with Reinvite and Cancel" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Your plan sets the seat limit. At the limit the form says "Seat limit reached" with an **Upgrade plan** link.
</Note>
