You'll answer a customer from the inbox. Open a conversation first.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Switch the AI off">
    In the conversation header, click **AI On** so it reads **AI Off**. Otherwise the agent may answer the customer's next message as well.

    <Visual note="Header toggle changing from AI On to AI Off" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type your reply">
    Click the composer at the bottom and type. Press Enter to send, or Shift+Enter for a new line.

    <Visual note="Composer with a message typed in" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Insert a quick reply">
    Type `/` to search your quick replies, then click the one you want. Edit it before you send.

    <Visual kind="gif" note="Typing / and choosing a quick reply" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach a file">
    Click the paperclip and choose one file. Images, video, and audio go as media; anything else goes as a document.

    <Visual note="Paperclip menu and a file attached" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reply to or react to a message">
    Hover over a message to quote it. On WhatsApp you can also react with one of eight emoji.

    <Visual note="Hover menu on a message" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close it when done">
    Click the close button in the header. The ticket moves to **Closed** and reopens if the customer writes again.

    <Visual note="Close button in the header" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
WhatsApp accepts free-form replies only within 24 hours of the customer's last message. After that, [start with a template](/guides/inbox/start-conversation).
</Warning>
