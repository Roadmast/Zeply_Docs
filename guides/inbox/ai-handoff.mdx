---
title: "Switch between AI and your team"
description: "Turn AI on or off for a ticket and take over a conversation."
---

import { Visual } from "/snippets/visual.mdx";

You'll take over a conversation from the agent, then hand it back.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the conversation">
    Click **Inbox** and open the ticket.

    <Visual note="Inbox with a ticket open" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn the AI off">
    In the header, click **AI On**. It changes to **AI Off** and the agent stops replying on this ticket.

    <Visual note="Header toggle on AI Off" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reply yourself">
    Type in the composer and send.

    <Visual note="Your reply sent in the conversation" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hand it back">
    When you're done, click **AI Off** to switch back to **AI On**. If the contact had been handed off, AI handling turns back on for them.

    <Visual note="Header toggle back on AI On" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Switch a contact from the contacts list

In **CRM Hub**, a contact's row has a **Handling** control. Change it between **AI Handled** and **Human Agent** to do the same for all of that contact's tickets.

<Visual note="Contacts table with the Handling control on a row" />

<Warning>
Nobody is notified inside Sayvy AI when the agent hands off. Watch the inbox, or send the `agent.handoff` webhook to a tool your team checks.
</Warning>
