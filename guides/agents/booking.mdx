You'll turn booking on for an agent and tell it how your appointments work. Connect [Google Calendar](/guides/connections/google-calendar) or [Calendly](/guides/connections/calendly) first.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Tools & Integrations">
    In the agent editor, click the **Tools & Integrations** tab.

    <Visual note="Tools & Integrations tab" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on Enable Booking">
    Switch on **Enable Booking**. A **Booking Instructions Prompt** box appears.

    <Visual note="Enable Booking on with the instructions box visible" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the booking instructions">
    Paste and edit:

    ```markdown
    Appointments are 30 minutes. We take bookings Monday to Saturday,
    10am to 7pm IST, up to two weeks ahead. Never book on Sundays.
    Before booking, confirm the customer's name and the treatment they want.
    Offer at most three times at once.
    ```

    <Visual note="Booking Instructions Prompt filled in" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the timezone">
    Click the **Prompts & Identity** tab and set **Timezone** to your business's.

    <Visual note="Timezone field" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and test">
    Click **Save Changes**, then **Test Agent**. Ask for an appointment and check your calendar.

    <Visual kind="gif" note="Test conversation that ends with the event on the calendar" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
The calendar isn't checked again when the slot is booked, so a double booking is possible. The customer also gets no calendar invite, so have the agent confirm the time in the chat.
</Warning>
