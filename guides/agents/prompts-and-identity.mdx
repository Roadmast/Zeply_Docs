You'll fill in the **Prompts & Identity** tab, the first tab of the agent editor.

<Visual note="Prompts & Identity tab, fully filled in" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the agent">
    Click **Agents**, then click your agent. The editor opens on **Prompts & Identity**.

    <Visual note="Agents list with an agent being clicked" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the basics">
    Enter the **Agent Name**, choose the **Primary Language**, and set the **Timezone** to your business's.

    <Visual note="Name, language, and timezone fields" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the welcome message">
    Type what the agent says first:

    ```text
    Hi, you've reached Green Leaf Dental. How can I help?
    ```

    <Visual note="Welcome Message field filled in" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the agent prompt">
    Click into **Agent prompt** and write who the agent is, its goal, and its rules:

    ```markdown
    ## Role
    You answer calls for Green Leaf Dental in Pune.

    ## Goal
    Help callers book a check-up. Collect their name, phone number, and preferred day.

    ## Rules
    - Never give a diagnosis. Suggest a visit instead.
    - If the caller is upset or asks for a person, hand over to a human.
    ```

    <Visual note="Agent prompt editor with the headings visible" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Changes**.

    <Visual note="Save Changes button" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Don't use **Insert Variables** in live agents yet. The tokens aren't filled in, so the agent reads them out as written.
</Warning>
