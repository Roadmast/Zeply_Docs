You'll choose the model that writes replies and the voice that speaks them, on the **AI Model & Voice** tab.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the tab">
    In the agent editor, click **AI Model & Voice**.

    <Visual note="AI Model & Voice tab" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a stack">
    Pick **Gemini**, or **Sarvam** if most callers speak an Indian language. Choose the stack before the voice, because switching stacks clears the voice.

    <Visual note="Stack selector with Gemini selected" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a category">
    Pick a category within the stack. Flash is a good start. The tab shows the reply rate and the voice price beside your choice.

    <Visual note="Category options with prices" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch on Enable voice">
    Turn on **Enable voice**. The voice list opens below.

    <Visual note="Enable voice switch on, voice list showing" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a voice">
    Search by name or gender, press play on a few, and click the one you want.

    <Visual kind="gif" note="Searching the voice list and playing a sample" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Changes**. You can't save with voice on and no voice picked.

    <Visual note="Save Changes button" />
  </Step>
</Steps>
