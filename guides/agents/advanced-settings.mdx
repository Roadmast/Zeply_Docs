You'll decide what is saved after a call, on the **Advanced Settings** tab. Do this before going live, because earlier calls aren't recorded afterwards.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the tab">
    In the agent editor, click **Advanced Settings**.

    <Visual note="Advanced Settings tab" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on recording">
    Switch on **Enable Voice / Call Recording**.

    <Visual note="Recording switch on" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on transcripts">
    Switch on **Enable Transcriptions**. It needs recording to be on.

    <Visual note="Transcriptions switch on" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a follow-up message">
    Switch on **Enable Follow Ups** and type the **Follow Up Message**:

    ```text
    Thanks for calling Green Leaf Dental. Reply here if you'd like to change your appointment.
    ```

    <Visual note="Follow Up Message field filled in" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the call length">
    Choose a value in **Max Call Duration (Minutes)**.

    <Visual note="Max Call Duration dropdown" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Changes**, then make a test WhatsApp call and open it in [Call Logs](/guides/call-logs).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Calls end at 15 minutes whatever you choose. Browser test calls aren't saved to call logs.
</Note>
