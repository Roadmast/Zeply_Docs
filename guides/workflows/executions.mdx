You'll read how real conversations moved through your workflow and find the step where people get stuck.

<Visual note="Executions tab with the summary counts and the graph" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Executions">
    In the builder, click the **Executions** tab.

    <Visual note="Builder header with Executions selected" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the summary">
    The top shows runs, completed runs, transfers, drop-offs, and the average number of turns.

    <Visual note="Summary counts" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter">
    Narrow the view by date range, channel, and version.

    <Visual note="Filters set to one channel and one version" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the drop-off">
    Each step shows how many runs entered and how many dropped off there. A step with a high drop-off is where callers get stuck.

    <Visual note="A step with a high drop-off number highlighted" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fix and republish">
    Go back to **Editor**, improve that step's prompt or transitions, and [publish](/guides/workflows/publish) again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
A run counts as dropped after 30 minutes without activity, if it never reached an ending or a transfer.
</Note>
