You'll add steps to the canvas, connect them, and set each one up. Open your workflow in the builder on the **Editor** tab first.

<Visual kind="gif" note="Adding a step, connecting it, and opening its settings" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a step">
    Click **Add Node**, or drag a step from the palette onto the canvas.

    <Visual note="Add Node button and the step palette" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect it">
    Drag from the handle on one step to the next step. An arrow appears between them.

    <Visual note="Arrow being drawn between two steps" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the step">
    Double-click the step. Its settings open with **Parameters** and **Transitions** tabs.

    <Visual note="Step settings panel, Parameters tab" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say what it does">
    In a **Greeting** or **Conversation** step, choose **Prompt** and type an instruction, such as "Ask what brings them in today". Choose **Static sentence** to say exact words instead.

    <Visual note="Conversation step with a prompt typed in" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write a transition">
    Open **Transitions**. Add a **When** with a plain-language condition, such as "The user wants to speak to a person". Choose **Always** to move on as soon as the step is done.

    <Visual note="Transitions tab with one When condition" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish with an Ending">
    Add an **Ending** step with a final message so every path has somewhere to stop.

    <Visual note="Ending step with a final message" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Every workflow has one **Start** step. A step with no transitions keeps the conversation where it is.
</Note>
