Use a separate workspace for each brand or client. This walkthrough creates one and switches between them.

<Visual kind="gif" note="Opening the workspace menu, creating a workspace, and switching back" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the workspace menu">
    Click the workspace name at the top of the left sidebar. A menu lists your workspaces.

    <Visual note="Sidebar with the workspace menu open" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create Workspace">
    Choose **Create Workspace**, type a name, and confirm.

    <Visual note="Create Workspace dialog with a name typed in" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Land in the new workspace">
    The sidebar switches to the new workspace. It starts empty, with no agents, contacts, or conversations.

    <Visual note="The new workspace, empty Agents page" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch back">
    Open the same menu and click the other workspace. Agents, inbox, and contacts change to match.

    <Visual note="Workspace menu with two workspaces listed" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Workspaces can't be renamed or deleted from the dashboard yet, so choose the name with care.
</Note>
