The **Connections** page has one card for each service you can link. This walkthrough shows the steps every card shares.

<Visual note="Connections page with all cards, some connected and some not" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Connections">
    Click **Connections** in the left sidebar.

    <Visual note="Sidebar with Connections highlighted" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your card">
    Scroll to the service you want: WhatsApp Business, Website Chatbot, Instagram Direct, Email Channel, Google Calendar, or Calendly.

    <Visual note="One unconnected card, with its connect button highlighted" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the connect button">
    Most cards send you to the provider to sign in and approve access, then bring you back.

    <Visual kind="gif" note="Clicking connect, approving at the provider, returning to a connected card" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the card">
    A connected card shows the account details, with buttons to update or disconnect.

    <Visual note="A connected card showing account details and the Disconnect button" />
  </Step>
</Steps>
