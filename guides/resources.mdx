# Organize workspace resources

Resources are the reusable building blocks behind your agents and workflows. Keep them easy to identify, properly scoped, and current.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Documents" icon="file-text" href="/guides/documents">Maintain source material and knowledge.</Card>
  <Card title="Connections" icon="plug" href="/guides/connections">Connect calendars, channels, and external services.</Card>
</CardGroup>

## Keep resources production-ready

Use descriptive names, separate test and production resources, remove unused access, and test any agent or workflow after a resource changes. A small resource inventory makes incident response much faster.
