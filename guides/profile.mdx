Your profile shows your name, email, and active workspace. You'll find it and change your password.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Click **Settings** in the left sidebar.

    <Visual note="Sidebar with Settings highlighted" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open User Profile">
    Click **User Profile**. Your name, email, and active workspace are shown.

    <Visual note="User Profile page" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change your password">
    Click your avatar and choose the password option from the menu.

    <Visual note="Avatar menu with the password option" />
  </Step>
</Steps>
