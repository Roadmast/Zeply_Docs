# Manage your profile

Open **Settings → Profile** to update the name and account details associated with your Sayvy user.

Keep your profile information accurate so teammates know who owns a conversation or change. Profile settings apply to you; workspace settings and member access are managed separately.

See [Team Members](/guides/team-members) for workspace access or [Security](/guides/security) for account protection.
