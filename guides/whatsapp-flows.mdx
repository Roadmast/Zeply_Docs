A Flow is a form with one or more screens that a customer fills in without leaving the chat. They live on the **WhatsApp Flows** page.

<Visual note="WhatsApp Flows list with name, category, and status columns" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open WhatsApp Flows">
    Click **WhatsApp Flows** from the dashboard.

    <Visual note="Dashboard with WhatsApp Flows highlighted" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse your Flows">
    The list shows each Flow's name, category, and status. Use the search box to find one.

    <Visual note="Search box and the list" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a Flow">
    Click a Flow to see its submissions.

    <Visual note="A Flow with its submissions table" />
  </Step>
</Steps>
